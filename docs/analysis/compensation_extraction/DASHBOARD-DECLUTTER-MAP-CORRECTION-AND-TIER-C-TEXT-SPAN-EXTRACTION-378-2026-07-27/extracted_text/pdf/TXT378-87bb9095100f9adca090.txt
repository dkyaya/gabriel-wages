Pavilion Reservations
(Memorial Weekend to Columbus Day Weekend)
The Town of Geddes Department of Parks and Recreation began accepting reservations for the use of the pavilion at Lakeland Park and Lindbergh Lawns Park on
January 3, 2022.
Lakeland Park is located on Alhan Parkway, just off State Fair Blvd. The facilities
available at Lakeland Park include a pavilion/electricity, restrooms, playground equipment, playing fields, tennis and basketball courts.
Lindbergh Lawns Park is located on Bennett Ave, just off Longbranch Road. The
facilities available include a small pavilion, restrooms, playground equipment, and halfcourt basketball.
Groups or organizations reserving the pavilions understand that they are entitled to
exclusive use of the pavilion, but not of all the other park facilities. They are still open
to the public.
Those wishing to reserve the pavilion must be Town of Geddes Residents and a
$40.00 user fee will be charged. For reservations, call the Department of Parks and
Recreation at (315) 487-8238.

Town of Geddes
Park Regulations
& Policies

In order to help keep our parks clean, safe and usable by all, Local Law 8 of the
year 2001, entitled “Park Regulations” was enacted to govern park use in the Town of
Geddes.
The law in its entirety can found in the Town of Geddes Code Book – Chapter 143.
Some of the regulations adopted are listed below:
Park Hours:
Sunrise to Sunset
Pavilion Use:
Memorial Weekend to Mid-October
Facilities and areas in the park shall be used only for the purposes designated or
implicit in their character.
No person shall carry into and/or have possession of any glass beverage containers.
It shall be unlawful to bring or cause to be brought any animal into any park,
recreation or playground area, except for trained guide dogs for the blind which are
expressly exempt from all provisions of the chapter.
Fires may not be built or allowed anywhere in park or playground areas except in
stoves, fireplaces, or designated areas provided for that purpose.
Licensed motor vehicles shall be permitted only on designated roadways, and vehicles shall be parked in areas of places so provided and designated.
Motor scooters, motorcycles, minibikes, go-carts, and snowmobiles shall not be
permitted.
Commit any act which is defined as criminal conduct under the New York State
Penal Law.
Any conduct which constitutes a violation of this chapter will be a violation, punishable by up to fifteen (15) days in jail and/or a fine of up to $250.00.
Each such violation shall constitute a separate violation punishable under this section.
HELP KEEP OUR PARKS CLEAN AND SAFE, if you see anyone misusing the
park - please let us know. To report any problems, etc. call the Geddes Police or the
Geddes Recreation Department.
****The Town of Geddes supports the county’s no smoking initiative in public
parks by adopting a “no smoking” policy in town parks. The enforcement of this policy
is through voluntary compliance.

The cold weather is upon us, here
are some safety tips to follow. Take
extra time when traveling and make
sure your vehicle is clear of snow and
ice. Your vehicles wiper blades
should be in good working order.
Make sure you have a blanket, extra
hats and gloves in your vehicle in the
event the vehicle becomes disabled. It
is always smart to wear a weather resistant coat while outside. Attempt to
avoid getting wet and being exposed
to the cold for extended periods of
time, as this could cause frostbite. It
is best to always plan ahead for the
possibility of extreme weather conditions.
Please be careful when shoveling
snow, be aware of the extra excursion
you are putting on your body. Your
body is already fighting to stay warm
and when shoveling you could have a
medical issue. Always make sure
someone knows you are outside in the
cold shoveling, so if you aren’t seen
for a bit they can come find you to
make sure you are o.k.
A reminder during these winter
months from November 1st to April
1st, 2021 between the hours of 2am
and 6am there is No Overnight Parking on the Town streets. This is to
allow plows to properly clean the
streets we travel on. Our Highway
Department does an amazing job during the winter months, so please help
them by making sure you don’t park
your vehicles on the streets during
that time frame. Thank You!

winter months. Many of us try different ways to heat our homes and stay
warm, so in the event of a failure or
issue a working smoke detector and
CO detector can save lives!

is the town code that it is not permissible to ‘plow, shovel, sweep or pile
snow from a private driveway in such
a manner as to deposit the same in the
public roadway or on a public rightof-way or sidewalk or across the
street from said driveway or onto the
property of another’.
Keep in mind that all new fences,
sheds (accessory structures), pools,
decks, etc. require a Building Permit,
even if you are replacing an existing
item. Building codes & zoning codes
change over the years and the current
codes must be addressed and complied with.
Complaint forms are available on
the Town’s website (as well as available in our office) to make it easier
for residents to file a complaint. We
will address all written complaints
submitted to our office. We request
all complaints are written on the form
provided with all information filled in
as we did last year. Your name, ad-

dress, phone number & signature are
required information for our office to
successfully investigate each complaint. Complaints are kept confidential, but we may need to contact you
for more information if we are unable
to locate what your concern was at a
parcel. Also, by signing the complaint
form, it gives us permission to enter
your yard to see an issue not visible
from the street, for example in a back
yard.
Basketball hoops should not be set
up in the road right-of-way. The
metal rims projecting out into the
street is a very dangerous situation
that a school bus, mail truck, garbage
truck, an emergency vehicle, etc.
could hit with their windshield. We
ask that you keep them in your driveway for safety reasons.
As always, we are happy to
answer any questions. Call 315468-3600 Ext.8 or email us at
dbalcer@townofgeddes.com.
