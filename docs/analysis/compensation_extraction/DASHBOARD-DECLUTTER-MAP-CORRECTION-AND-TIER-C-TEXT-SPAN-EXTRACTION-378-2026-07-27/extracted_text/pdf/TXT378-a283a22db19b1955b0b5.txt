TOWN ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT
2025 Proposed Wage Matrix Adjustment For Regular Non-Union
MEETING DATE:

04/28/2025

REQUESTED BY:

TOWN ADMINISTRATOR

ISSUE:
Request approval of the proposed wage matrix adjustment for regular non-union personnel townwide, except for per diem personnel.
BACKGROUND INFORMATION:
•

In 2023, the Town consulted with Pay Point HR to develop an updated wage matrix and labor
grades for non-union personnel.

•

The final matrix was implemented on 07/01/2024.

•

The final report from the consultant stated, “It is recommended that in subsequent years after
the adoption of the recommended pay plan, the Town should adjust the entire salary scale by
the CPI annually as the budget permits.” This is needed to keep the matrix relevant to the
market and help the Town attract and retain highly qualified personnel.

FINANCIAL IMPACT:
The 3.3% matrix adjustment was included in the 2025 operating budget which passed at Town
Meeting. Subsequently the cost of the wage adjustment, as well as any applicable step increases,
are included in the various wage and benefit lines for each department.
RECOMMENDATION:
Motion to accept the proposed 3.3% wage matrix adjustment for regular non-union personnel
effective 07/01/2025.
