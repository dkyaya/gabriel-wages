ORDINANCE 023-043
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 32.62 OF THE CITY OF
SHEPHERDSVILLE CODE OF ORDINANCES RELATED TO TENTATIVE
AGREEMENT UNDER COLLECTIVE BARGAINING PROGRAM FOR POLICE
OFFICERS.
SUMMARY
1.

Sets forth that Section 32.62(B) of the Code of Ordinances is amended to
reflect that upon reaching a tentative collective bargaining agreement an
ordinance containing the collective bargaining contract shall be presented
to the City Council no sooner than the first day of January for its action
and that upon passage and approval of such ordinance, the collective
bargaining agreement shall become a contract binding on all parties for a
term not to exceed two (2) fiscal years.

2.

Provides for repeal of inconsistent laws, severability of provisions, and an
effective date upon passage and publication.

Public Notices are very important...
Did you know that only the property owners who abut a tract which is asking for a rezoning
are required to get a letter from the planning commission? And there is no requirement to
keep posting signs on the property in question if they are stolen or knocked down by weather. Zoning notices are run from 7-21 days prior to the county planning commission
hearing in The Pioneer News.
A subscription and looking at the legal section could be a whole lot cheaper than
getting into a legal battle over a zoning case later.
