AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE FOR THE BUDGET OF THE CITY OF TEGA CAY STORM
WATER UTILITY DEPARTMENT (TCSUD) FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING
OCTOBER 1, 2014, AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2015, TO PROVIDE FOR THE RECEIPT
AND EXPENDITURE OF FUNDS, AND OTHER MATTERS RELATED HERETO.
BE IT ORDAINED by the Mayor and City Council of the City of Tega Cay duly assembled:
SECTION 1- Annual TCSUD Budget. For the purposes of providing for the orderly administration of
TCSUD operations, there is hereby enacted an annual budget for all revenues for TCSUD, for the Fiscal Year
2014-2015, commencing October 1, 2014, and ending September 30, 2015. The Annual TCSUD Budget for
Fiscal Year 2014-2015 is appended hereto and made a part of this Ordinance.

SECTION 2 - Revenue. In order to provide services to the customers of TCSUD and to provide for the
public health and welfare of our community, the TCSUD FY 2014-2015 anticipates and forecasts the
following revenue and revenue sources for the TCSUD Fund:

SECTION 3 – Charges and Fees. Pursuant to South Carolina Code, the City of Tega Cay Storm Water
Utility Department will assess customers of TCSUD service charges and fees necessary to support TCSUD
operations and development needs as prescribed by City Council and City staff. Such service charges and fees
are included on the attached Exhibit A.

SECTION 4 - TCSUD Fee Billing. Annual billings will be mailed and collected pursuant to applicable
City Ordinances.

SECTION 5 - Receipt of Funds. The City Finance Director, or his/her designee, shall deposit TCSUD
Account Funds paid to the account of TCSUD in such bank accounts as deemed advisable. TCSUD funds
received by any other official or employee of the City shall be paid over daily to the City Finance Director, or
his/her designee. The City Finance Director, or his/her designee, shall establish an accounting system which
shall ensure the proper segregation of funds prescribed by this Ordinance and further shall segregate all funds
received from special projects, state or federal programs, if so required, so that such funds are not co-mingled
with funds received pursuant to this Ordinance for the general operation of TCSUD.

SECTION 6 - Accounting. The City Staff is directed to maintain special projects, state or federal programs
in a separate and segregated account, which may be transferred to the Storm Water Utility Fund as needed. The
City Staff shall adhere to the regulations of the United States Department of the Treasury with respect to the
administration and expenditure of such funds.

SECTION 7 - Distribution of Funds.

All funds shall be disbursed in accordance with the procurement

procedures of the City of Tega Cay and in accordance with pertinent federal and state laws and regulations.

SECTION 8 - Budget Year and Closing of Accounts. The budget year shall expire on September 30,
2015. No funds shall be disbursed pursuant to this Ordinance unless such funds have been obligated prior to the
close of the budget year. Within the meaning of this Section, the term "obligated" shall be deemed to include
the placing of an order or the signing of a contract for the delivery of goods or services. In the event that funds
are obligated during the budget year, they must be actually expended and disbursed within sixty (60) days from
the closing of the budget year.

SECTION 10 - Fiscal Administration. TCSUD warrants that all applicable City, State and Federal Codes
will be followed in the administration of the FY 2014-2015 TCSUD budget. These Codes include, but are not
limited to, regulation of borrowing funds, fiscal controls, independent auditing, and the purchase of goods and
services. Should there arise a need to derivate from City code to meet TCSUD needs, no derivation from City
codes will be implemented without the prior consent, through formal action, of City Council.

SECTION 11 - Power of City Council. The City Council may authorize the transfer of funds within any
of TCSUD’s various accounts by resolution. Pursuant to the South Carolina Code of Law, the City Council may
authorize TCSUD to incur long-term indebtedness through bonds, leases or other legally acceptable instruments.
SECTION 12 - Date Effective. This Ordinance shall become effective October 1, 2014 and shall
expire on September 30, 2015.
This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its final passage.
