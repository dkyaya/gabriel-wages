ORDINANCE NUMBER 2024-03
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF MT. WASHINGTON, KENTUCKY CONCERNING THE
CREATION OF A COLLECTIVE BARGAINING PROGRAM WITH THE MT. WASHINGTON
LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS AND THE CITY OF MT. WASHINGTON, KENTUCKY
WHEREAS, THE CITY COUNCIL HAS DETERMINED THE ADVANTAGES OF
COLLECTIVE BARGAINING WITH THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS:
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF MT.
WASHINGTON, KENTUCKY as follows:
SECTION I
The City of Mt. Washington City Council hereby establishes a policy of the City of Mt. Washington
to participate in collective bargaining with duly authorized representatives of Mt. Washington,
Kentucky police officers. To effect that policy, the following procedures shall be utilized.
SECTION II
A. In the event a petition for recognition as collective bargaining representative of a group of
police officers of the Mt. Washington Police Department is presented to the Mayor, the Mayor
shall cause to be conducted a preliminary investigation to verify that such petition is bona fide
and that it presents a question whether the petitioning organization represents the officers of
the Police Department.
B. If the preliminary investigation indicates that the petition is bona fide and presents a question
whether the petitioning organization represents the police officers, the Mayor shall cause to be
conducted a secret ballot election among the officers.
C. If such election determines that the police officers desire to be represented in collective
bargaining by the petitioning organization, the Mayor shall present those results to the City
Council. The City Council may then pass an ordinance recognizing the petitioning organization
as the exclusive bargaining representative of the unit of officers.
D. Not more than one such election for any group of officers shall be conducted in any one
calendar year. Once a union or other organization is so recognized, such recognition shall
continue in force from year to year thereafter unless repealed by validly passed and signed
ordinance.
SECTION III
A. Following passage and approval of an ordinance recognizing an organization as the collective
bargaining representative of a unit of police officers, the Mayor shall act as negotiator on behalf
of the City of Mt. Washington and meet with representatives of the union or organization for
the purpose of negotiating a collective bargaining agreement regarding the terms and
conditions of employment of the police officers in the unit. The parties shall meet and bargain
in good faith to reach such an agreement.

B. Upon the reaching of a tentative agreement regarding a collective bargaining agreement, an
ordinance containing a collective bargaining agreement shall be presented to the City Council
for its action, and, on passage and approval of such ordinance, it shall become a contract
binding on all parties for a term not to exceed the biennium or the existing term of the
incumbent members of the City Council. Upon expiration of a collective bargaining agreement,
the parties shall negotiate in good faith for a new agreement.
SECTION IV
Petitions for decertification of a recognized collective bargaining representative shall be processed
in a manner consistent with the process for recognition set forth above, subject to the limitations
that (a) no decertification election shall be conducted among officers of the same unit within a year
of an election for recognition or any other election for decertification, and (b) no election shall be
conducted during the term of a collective bargaining agreement applicable to the unit of officers
in question.
SECTION V
Officers at or above the rank of Lieutenant Colonel or Major shall not be included in the unit of
officers represented by a union or other organization for collective bargaining purposes.
SECTION VI
Strikes and slowdowns by officers of the Mt. Washington Police Department shall be strictly
prohibited, and shall, without waiver of any other remedies or relied, constitute grounds for
immediate termination.
SECTION VII
This Ordinance shall be published according to law.
The effective date of this Ordinance shall be the date on which the Ordinance is published
according to law.
Should any part of this Ordinance, including but not limited to, any section, subsection, paragraph,
sentence, line, clause, phrase, or any other portion of this Ordinance, is declared illegal or
unconstitutional, or otherwise invalid by a Court of competent jurisdiction, such declaration shall
not affect the remaining portions hereof. Any agreement currently in effect shall remain in effect
in accordance with the terms of the agreement and this, and all applicable, ordinances.
The City Clerk is hereby authorized to publish this ordinance in accordance with the Kentucky
Revised Statutes.
