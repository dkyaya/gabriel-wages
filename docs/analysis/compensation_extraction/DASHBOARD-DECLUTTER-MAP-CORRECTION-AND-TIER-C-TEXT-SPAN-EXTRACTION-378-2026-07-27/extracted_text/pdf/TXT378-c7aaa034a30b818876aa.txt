Resolution 2012-201

“Resolution Calling for Restoration of Energy Receipts
Funding to Municipalities”

Council President Gennaro presented Resolution 2012-201 calling for
Restoration of Energy Receipts funding to Municipalities. He explained the
Borough, along with the other Municipalities Municipalities, has lost out on
millions of dollars since the State took over the collection of energy taxes. He
explained the State is supposed to reimburse the municipalities, but the amounts
have become decreasingly smaller over time. He stated this Resolution asks for
the collection of these taxes to fall back into the hands of the Municipalities.
