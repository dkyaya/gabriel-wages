Resolution #2020-0429 -Bernards Township is Granting a Hardship Waiver to Asya Hopps for the Sale of
her Affordable Unit known as 4109 Hackleberry Square Block 9001 Lot 41.11
WHEREAS, Asya Hopps, single (the “Homeowner”) is the owner of property designated as Lot 41.11 in Block 9001
(formerly identified as Lot 41.09 in Block 178.03) on the Tax Map of the Township of Bernards in the County of Somerset,
State of New Jersey, commonly known as 4109 Hackleberry Square (the “Property”), which was purchased pursuant
to an Affordable Housing Agreement; and
WHEREAS, the Homeowner requested to sell her one-bedroom moderate unit on or about July 21, 2020, authorizing
the Administrative Agent for the Township to refer certified income-eligible potential buyers, and set a maximum
resale price of $142,683.06; and
WHEREAS, an owner of a low/moderate income unit may request that the unit be sold to a household whose income
exceeds the established income eligibility criteria; and
WHEREAS, the Homeowner has made a good faith effort to sell the unit to a certified household and a certified
household signed a contract to purchase her unit on October 3, 2020 for $135,000 plus $3000 towards closing costs,
$10,000 below her asking price; and
WHEREAS, the Homeowner has already purchased another home, but the buyer was unable to obtain a mortgage
and the contract of sale was cancelled on December 7, 2020; and
WHEREAS, the Administrative Agent has recommended that pursuant to N.J.A.C. 5:80-26.14(a) 13 that permits an
Administrative Agent to process requests and make determinations on requests by owners of restricted units for
hardship waivers and the Hardship Waiver be granted, and the Township Attorney has reviewed the Homeowners
request for a Hardship Waiver; and

TOWNSHIP COMMITTEE MINUTES

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT the Township Committee of Bernards Township, County of
Somerset, hereby grants the Hardship Waiver to Asya Hopps for the sale of her unit known as 4109 Hackleberry
Square; and
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED THAT the Hardship Waiver is granted only to Asya Hopps
and all subsequent owners are subject to all restrictions as described in the Affordable Housing Agreement.

Resolution #2020-0430- Extension of Resolution #2020-0306 Permitting Relief of Certain Ordinances to
Assist Local Businesses as they Re-Open Following the Lifting of COVID-19 Executive Orders
Motion by Carpenter, second by Fields, that Resolution #2020-0430 be approved.
Roll Call:
Aye:
Carpenter, Esposito, Fields, Harris, Baldassare, Jr.
Nay:
Abstain:
Motion carried.
WHEREAS, the COVID-19 pandemic continues to affect public health and therefore continues to effect local
businesses; and
WHEREAS, certain ordinances were relaxed and granted relief from enforcement to assist local businesses as they
responded to, altered, or re-opened their business operations in compliance with COVID-19 executive orders; and
WHEREAS, any such activity covered by a “Special Use Permit” authorized by Resolution #2020-0222, effective
through November 1, 2020, and extended by Resolution #2020-0306, effective through June 30th, 2021 unless
further extended by Resolution of the Township Committee.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Township Committee of the Township of Bernards
recognizes that the need continues to exist to extend this provision to assist local businesses and hereby extends
Resolution #2020-0306 permitting relief of certain ordinances through December 31, 2021.
Baldassare recused himself and left the dais. Carpenter presided over the meeting since Esposito was participating
by telephone.
