Ridley Township Board of Commissioners
April 27, 2022
Meeting Minutes
The Regular Meeting of the Ridley Township Board of Commissioners was called to order
by President Willert at 6:00pm on April 27, 2022 at the Township Building.
The following Commissioners were present: Mr. Saraceni, Mrs. Morrisette, Mrs. Cummins,
Mr. McMenamin, Mr. Saunders, Mr. McCrea, Mr. Willert, Mrs. Melasecca and Mr. Bidoli.
Also present were Mr. Ryan (Township Manager), Mr. Neill (Solicitor) and Mr. Catania
(Township Engineer).
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited.
Mr. Willert held a moment of silence and acknowledged the passing of Fred Sach, long-time
leader of Woodlyn Boy Scout Troop in Ridley Township.
Public Forum
John Shaffer, 309 Kossuth Ave., Milmont Park, appeared before the Board concerning a
manhole on MacDade Boulevard, the embankment on MacDade Boulevard by Raymour and
Flanigan, Ace Transmission property on Chester Pike, Eddie’s Market on Buchanan Avenue
and a commercial truck on Milmont Avenue.
Manager’s Report
Mr. Ryan requested ratification of the bid to award the 2011 Jeep Grand Cherokee through
Municibid in the amount of $8,200.00.
Motion made by Mr. McCrea, seconded by Mr. McMenamin to award the vehicle to the
highest bidder as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Ryan requested the Board ratify the 2022 through 2024 Ridley Township Police FOP
Contract which calls for wage increases of 4%, 2%, 2% for each year respectively, and also
provides for an Act 44 retirement program (DROP).
Motion made by Mr. McMenamin, seconded by Mrs. Cummins to ratify the contract as
presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Ryan reported the township received two requests from Notre Dame de Lourdes – one
for their food truck festival on May 20th and the other for their annual carnival in October.
Motion made by Mrs. Cummins, seconded by Mr. Saraceni to approve both requests
as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).

April 27, 2022 Meeting Minutes

The Board discussed allocating approximately $2 million ARPA funds to fully fund or
supplement stormwater quality projects, storm drainage project and sanitary sewer
projects. (A full list of projects is available on the township’s website.) Further funding will
be discussed at future meetings.
Motion made by Mr. Saunders, seconded by Mr. McMenamin to approve the ARPA
funding allocation as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Recycling Report
Mrs. Melasecca reported trash collected in March was 1,187.61 tons and recycling collected
in March was 202.03 tons. 23 televisions were collected in March.
Controller’s Report – No Report
Solicitor’s Report
Mr. Neill reported a tax assessment appeal for 2021 was filed by the homeowner at 500 W.
Sellers Avenue. An appraisal was done and the parties reached a fair market value which
would reduce the township taxes by 2,748.00.
Motion made by Mrs. Cummins, seconded by Mr. Bidoli to approve the settlement
and authorize the stipulation to be signed as presented. Motion carried unanimously
(9-0).
Engineer’s Report
Mr. Catania recommended approval of the following certificates: Radio Communications
Service – 2022 Police Radio Maintenance - $3,329.01; A to U Services, Inc. for the 2022
Storm & Sanitary Sewer Repair Contract - $21,943.55; N. Abbonizio Contractors –
PENNVEST Stormwater Improvements - $325,538.88; N. Abbonizio Construction Inc. –
Greenhouse Drive Sanitary Sewer - $92,873.90; Mobile Dredging & Video Pipe, Inc. –
Braxton Road Sanitary Sewer Rehabilitation Project Change Order #1; Frania, Inc. – Nall

Motion made by Mr. McCrea, seconded by Mrs. Cummins to approve the certificates
as read. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Catania requested approval of an escrow agreements for Leonard Builders at 317
Gorsuch Street in the amount of $15,796.00 and Kim Broomall for Adams Street
development in the amount of $9,557.66.
Motion made by Mr. McCrea, seconded by Mr. McMenamin to approve the escrow
approvals as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
2
April 27, 2022 Meeting Minutes

Mr. Catania reported the bids received for the Muhlenberg Avenue culvert project came in
over the budget estimate and requested the bids be rejected and re-advertised.
Motion made by Mr. Saunders, seconded by Mrs. Cummins to reject the bids and readvertise. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Catania reported four bids were received for the 2022 Road Program. He
recommended award to Glasgow, Inc. at their base bid of $758,538.00 plus an alternate of
$76,157.50 with concurrence of Darby Township and Glenolden Borough since it is a
shared street.
Motion made by Mrs. Cummins, seconded by Mr. Bidoli to award the bid as
presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Catania requested authorization to advertise for the 9th Avenue Drainage Improvement
Project.
Motion made by Mrs. Cummins, seconded by Mr. Saraceni to authorize the
advertisement as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Catania announced DELCORA will be holding a meeting in Garling Hall on April 28th
concerning their proposed tunnel project in the Route 291 area of the township.
Mr. Catania updated the Board on the PENNVEST Project. The contractor getting toward
the end of the project. Sixth & Stanbridge is currently underway, Armstrong & Wendy has
been delayed due to on-going supply issues and restoration work has begun.
Highway/Sanitation Committee Report
Mr. McCrea announced there will be no trash collection on May 17th, Primary Election Day.
Law & Ordinance Committee Report
Mr. Neill and the Board discussed the proposed Medical Marijuana Facilities Ordinance and
the public hearing scheduled for this proposed ordinance on May 19th.
Motion made by Mr. Bidoli, seconded by Mr. McMenamin announcing their intent to
adopt the Medical Marijuana Facilities Ordinance as presented at a future meeting.
Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Neill and the Board discussed the proposed Resolution increasing electrical permit fees
for non-residential projects from $55 per $1,000 of cost for the first $250,000 of cost to $60
per $1,000 of cost for the first $250,000 of cost.
Motion made by Mr. Saunders, seconded by Mr. Bidoli to approve the Resolution as
presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).

Mrs. Cummins reported on upcoming events at the library. 4,769 patrons visited the

Finance Committee Report
Mrs. Morrisette reported all Commissioners received a list of this month’s bills.
Motion made by Mrs. Morrisette, seconded by Mr. Saraceni that the bills be approved
subject to the Controller’s review and approval. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Old and New Business
Mr. Saunders thanked Woodlyn Troop 43 for cleaning up the creek behind the High School.
He stated there were about 17 volunteers and they did a great job.
Mr. McCrea reminded everyone to slow down while driving. Summer is fast approaching
and kids are out and about. He also thanked all the volunteers in every organization for
what they do.
Mr. McMenamin discussed the ARPA funding and how the projects that were approved will
make a difference.
Mrs. Morrisette thanked the Highway Department and the coaches and parents who helped
get Blackrock Park ready for Opening Day.
Mr. Bidoli thanked the Police Department for everything they do.
Adjournment
Motion made by Mr. Willert to adjourn the meeting, seconded by Mr. McMenamin.
Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Meeting concluded at 6:46pm.
