COLLECTIVE LABOR AGREEMENT
AGREEMENT made this 18th
___ day of April 2023 between the CITY OF BOISE, a
municipal corporation of Idaho, hereinafter referred to as BOISE CITY, and the
INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF FIRE FIGHTERS LOCAL 149, hereinafter referred
to as the UNION.
W I T N E S S E T H:
ARTICLE 1. PREAMBLE
WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of Idaho Code, Chapter 18, Title 44
(Session laws 1970, Chapter 138 as amended), BOISE CITY and the UNION have
reached agreement with respect to wages, rates of pay, working conditions and all other
terms and conditions of employment pertaining to the members employed by BOISE CITY
in various capacities.
WHEREAS, BOISE CITY and the UNION agree that the establishment and
maintenance of trust, harmony, efficiency, and job effectiveness are in the best interests
of BOISE CITY, the UNION and the public. Furthermore, it is agreed by both BOISE CITY
and the UNION that the successful resolution of problems is an important element of their
working relationship, and BOISE CITY and the UNION commit themselves to the
equitable and peaceful adjustment of any differences which may arise.
WHEREAS, BOISE CITY agrees to recognize the Department's Fire Fighters as
professional Fire Fighters dedicated to serving the citizens of Boise City; the UNION
agrees to support BOISE CITY in improving the safety of citizens and Fire Fighters to the
fullest extent possible.
WHEREAS, representatives of the UNION timely presented to BOISE CITY written
notice for a meeting for collective bargaining purposes pursuant to the provisions of said
Chapter 18, Title 44, Idaho Code (Session Laws 1970, Chapter 138); and
WHEREAS, representatives of BOISE CITY and the UNION have met and
conferred in good faith regarding wages, rates of pay, working conditions and all terms
and conditions of employment, and, as a result thereof, BOISE CITY and the UNION
desire to enter into a Collective Labor Agreement resolving such matters in favor of the
UNION and BOISE CITY for the period of October 1, 2022 through September 30, 2026.

ARTICLE 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS
Section A. Term.
This Collective Labor Agreement, hereinafter referred to and called AGREEMENT,
shall become effective October 1, 2022 and shall remain in full force and effect through
September 30, 2026.
In agreeing to this forty-eight (48) month AGREEMENT, the parties agree that the
statutory notice provisions of Idaho Code §44-1804 and §44-1808 are separate and
distinct and further, that for purposes of preparation for the negotiation of a successor
AGREEMENT, the UNION may give timely notice of request for bargaining on matters
requiring appropriation pursuant to Idaho Code §44-1808, and may thereafter give
separate timely notice of request for a meeting for collective bargaining purposes at least
one hundred and fifty (150) days prior to the expiration of this AGREEMENT.
BOISE CITY agrees to the following conditions with respect to the negotiation of a
successor labor agreement:
1. That the amount specifically designated for the Fire Department of BOISE CITY in the
BOISE CITY's approved Budget for the 2026 fiscal year shall not alone be a limiting
consideration in satisfying the fiscal needs of a successor agreement addressing the
remainder of that fiscal year after the expiration of this AGREEMENT on September
30, 2026; and
2. That serious, good faith consideration will be given to the application of all surplus
funds, undesignated funds, reserves and fund equity in satisfying the fiscal needs of
a successor agreement addressing all fiscal periods covered by the successor
agreement.
Section B. Scope.
BOISE CITY hereby recognizes the UNION as the sole and exclusive bargaining
agent for all Fire Fighters in the Fire Department of BOISE CITY pursuant to Chapter 18,
Title 44, Idaho Code. For purposes of this AGREEMENT, the Fire Fighters in the Fire

Section D. Discrimination.
1. BOISE CITY and the UNION shall not discriminate against any member with respect
to a member's compensation, terms, conditions or privileges of employment because
of:
a. Activity on behalf of, or membership in, the UNION; or,
b. BOISE CITY is committed to providing equal employment opportunity for all
persons without regard to race, color, religion, gender, age, national origin,
sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, veteran status, or any other
applicable legally protected status for which reasonable accommodations
can be made; or,
c. Other factors which do not constitute bonafide occupational requirements.
It is the exclusive responsibility of BOISE CITY to determine bonafide
occupational requirements.
2. Grievance settlements relative to this provision, or arbitration awards if any, shall be
reduced by any amounts obtained by the affected member as a result of any
administrative action before a Local, State or Federal Agency concerning the same
complaint, but only to the extent of cumulative damages pertaining to the same types
of relief obtained.

Section E. Residency.
Residency within the corporate limits of Boise City shall not be a condition of
employment or continued employment. However, if non-residency creates circumstances
affecting the operating efficiency or response capabilities of the Fire Department of
BOISE CITY, residency shall be a subject of renegotiation between the UNION and
BOISE CITY. The domiciling of Fire Department of BOISE CITY vehicles shall be within
a 21-mile distance from BOISE CITY limits for all vehicle assignments.
Section F. Union Business.
Up to three (3) members of the UNION's Contract Negotiation Committee shall be
allowed time off for all meetings with BOISE CITY for contract negotiations which shall be
mutually set by BOISE CITY and the UNION. In addition, the UNION may bring other
requests for time off for UNION conventions, seminars, and workshops and other
meetings of a similar nature to the attention of the Fire Chief. Where it is demonstrated
that attendance at such meetings will benefit BOISE CITY, the Fire Chief may, in his
discretion, grant time off with Composite Pay. BOISE CITY agrees to grant time off to
UNION officials when necessary to conduct UNION business with Fire Department of
BOISE CITY management and BOISE CITY officials.
Members of the UNION involved in lobbying during Legislative sessions shall be
granted reasonable time off for such activity that is beneficial to BOISE CITY and the fire
service whenever staffing is such that there will be no additional cost to BOISE CITY.
Section G. Semi-Monthly Service Charge.
The UNION shall request present or future bargaining unit members to pay to the
UNION a semi-monthly service charge. For UNION members that service charge will
constitute that member's UNION dues, fees and assessments. The UNION shall request
each bargaining unit member to sign and file with BOISE CITY a written authorization
approving this deduction, and remittance, from wages.
BOISE CITY agrees to deduct authorized semi-monthly service charges in
amounts specified by the authorized officer of the UNION, from the pay of bargaining unit
members upon receipt of the written authorization from the employee. BOISE CITY
further agrees to transmit those amounts monthly to the UNION on or about the first day
of the month following such semi-monthly service charges are withheld from the wage of

gives written notice to BOISE CITY of revocation of the employee's authorization.
The UNION agrees to give BOISE CITY a minimum of thirty (30) days written

charge.
Section H. Strikes.
Upon the consummation and during the term of this AGREEMENT, no member
covered by this AGREEMENT shall strike or recognize a picket line of any labor
organization while in the performance of the member's official duty.
Section I. Management Rights.
BOISE CITY shall retain the exclusive right to exercise the regular and customary
functions of management, including but not limited to directing the activities of the Fire
Department of BOISE CITY, determining levels of service and methods of operation,
including contracting of Fire Department of BOISE CITY services to other agencies; the
introduction of new equipment; the right to hire, lay-off, transfer and promote; to discipline
and to discharge its members for cause; to determine work schedules and assign work;
to determine the amount of apparatus in the main and reserve fleet; and to take such
actions as necessary during a local emergency. Local emergency shall include but not be
limited to natural disasters, epidemics, terrorist attacks, or other event deemed a local
emergency by BOISE CITY. If after thirty (30) days a state of emergency is still in effect,
the UNION may exercise the right to meet with BOISE CITY to discuss the necessity of
any changes made to operations during the state of emergency. Provided, that nothing in
this Article shall nullify: (1) Any provisions elsewhere in this AGREEMENT, or, (2) BOISE
CITY's statutory obligation to negotiate with the UNION pursuant to Chapter 18, Title 44,
Idaho Code.
Section J. Prevailing Rights.
All rights, benefits and privileges held by members which are not included in this
AGREEMENT shall remain in force, unchanged and unaffected in any manner.

eighteen (18) courses) or an equivalent combination of both listed in Procedure
351.32A.

two (2) “A” courses or six (6) “B” courses or an equivalent combination of both listed
in Procedure 351.32A. The Captain will receive a Step 2 increase upon successful
completion of an additional two (2) “A” courses (for a total of four (4) courses) or an

Section C. Longevity Pay
Members shall receive a fifty-five hundredths’ percent (.55%) increase of the
member’s Base Rate for each subsequent qualifying year of service in the Fire
Department of BOISE CITY, recognizing the increasing proficiency which accrues with
increased years of service. Members shall start receiving this increase in the first payroll
period following the qualified member’s anniversary date with the Fire Department of
BOISE CITY.
Section D. Overtime.
1. Overtime shall be compensated at the Overtime Rate.
a. Up to thirty (30) minutes shall be deemed to be one-half (½) hour overtime.
b. Over thirty (30) minutes in each hour shall be deemed one (1) hour
overtime.
2. At the request of the member, the shift Battalion Chief may provide that, in lieu of
monetary payment for overtime, the member will earn compensatory time at one and
one-half (1.5) times hours worked. Otherwise, all overtime earned by a member will
be paid as part of their regular paycheck at the Overtime Rate. A member, may receive
payment of some or all accumulated compensatory time as part of the annual selldown
outlined in Article 4. MEMBER BENEFITS; Section A. Vacations; Subsection 8.
Annual Selldown.

3. The maximum total accrual for compensatory time shall at no time exceed four
hundred (400) hours.
4. A member ordered to remain on duty pending the arrival of the member's relief shall
be paid at the Overtime Rate from the regular shift change time until properly relieved.
5. Overtime shall be distributed as follows:
a. Overtime, in the Operations Division, shall be distributed as equally as
practicable, except:
i. To fill a position in an emergency situation.
ii. For staffing requirements for Paramedic Engines, the Dive
Company, the Technical Rescue Company, the Hazardous Materials
Company, and the ARFF Company when a sufficient number of
certified members are not available.
iii. For three (3) hour blocks of leave.
b. Overtime for all other Divisions shall be first distributed to those members
within the same rank and Division as equally as practicable. For all Divisions
except Operations, a list of qualified members shall be established for
overtime consideration. The distribution of overtime shall be based upon the
need of the department.
6. FLSA Overtime. For calculation of FLSA Overtime, the hours paid will be calculated
at the FLSA Overtime Rate. The Standard FLSA period is 182 hours (in a 24-day
cycle). Sick Leave, Kelly Days, Court and Jury Leave, Military Leave, Service Sick,
Extended Sick Leave, Leave Without Pay, and/or Bereavement Leave used by a
member during the FLSA period shall not count as hours worked when making the
calculation.
Section E. Call Back.
Call back shall be paid at the Overtime Rate and shall start once the member has
received the call to report to work, so long as the member leaves his or her present
location to proceed to the emergency call back location. In accordance with Article 3.
Wages; Section D. Overtime, a member called to work at a time other than that member's
regularly scheduled shift shall accrue a minimum of three (3) hours at the Overtime Rate
unless such time is continuous with his/her scheduled work-day. In that case, the member

12

shall be paid for the actual call back hours worked to the nearest one-half (½) hour at the
Overtime Rate. On-Call Incident Safety Officers that are called back, respond to an
emergency location, or are cancelled enroute, shall be paid for the actual call back hours
worked to the nearest one-half (½) hour at the Overtime Rate.
Section F. Working Out of Classification (WOC).
A member covered by this AGREEMENT shall, when authorized to carry out the
duties of a position of a higher rank, receive the difference in pay between the average
Standard Rate of all members in the rank that member fills and the average Standard
Rate of all members in the rank of the member’s current rank. For members in the Fire
Fighter rank the additional pay shall be the difference between the average Standard
Rate of all members in the rank that the member fills and the average Standard Rate of
all members in the Fire Fighter I, II, and III rank combined. This Rate will be re-calculated
and applied annually in conjunction with the first month in the new fiscal year. Working
out of class is included in the FLSA Overtime Rate. Battalion Chiefs may not work out of
rank as Senior Fire Fighter or Fire Fighter.
In the absence of the Fire Marshal Division Chief, the Assistant Fire Marshal or the
Assistant Fire Chief or a Captain Prevention authorized to carry out the duties of the Fire
Marshal Division Chief will assume the duties of the Fire Marshal Division Chief. When a
Captain Prevention assume the duties, they shall receive the difference in pay between
the average Standard Rate of all the members in the Captain Prevention rank and the
Fire Marshal Division Chief. A Captain Prevention shall not be compensated at the above
rate for call back standby or for overtime spent performing their Captain Prevention duties.
Section G. Mileage for Station Changes.
If any member is required to use a personal vehicle to change that member's
working place from that to which the member reported for duty, the member shall be
entitled to mileage at the prevailing BOISE CITY rate for the distance traveled. Payment
for travel mileage shall be included on the member’s regular paycheck.
Section H. Court and Jury Leave.
Any member who is directed by proper authority to serve on jury duty, or to appear

all quarters shown in the member’s accrual each January. Shift Duty Members shall earn
twenty-four (24) hours for each Kelly Day and Forty (40) Hour Workweek Members shall
earn eight (8) hours for each Kelly Day. Each member will be compensated at the
Composite Rate for Kelly Days earned but not taken, or charged at the Composite Rate
for Kelly Days taken but not earned, in the event of termination, layoff or reduction in
force. Kelly Days not used during the calendar year in which earned shall not carry over
to the following year. Kelly Days must be taken as an entire Shift for Shift Duty Members
or eight (8) hours for Forty (40) Hour Workweek Members.
A conversion of twenty-four (24) hours to eight (8) hours for the current balance
shall be used for Shift Duty Members transferring to an assigned forty (40) hour workweek
schedule; or, if on modified duty, upon usage. A conversion of eight (8) hours to twentyfour (24) hours for the current balance shall be used for Forty (40) Hour
Workweek Members transferring to an assigned shift duty schedule, or if the hours were
converted while on modified duty.

Section F. Work Duties.
Except as otherwise provided by this AGREEMENT, every day of the week shall
be a standard work-day with the duties and training to be performed by members as
assigned by the Battalion Chief.
Saturday work duties shall ordinarily include only apparatus, equipment, and

non-emergency calls. Sunday work duties shall ordinarily include only apparatus,
equipment, and station maintenance, individual study, company briefings, physical fitness
and response to all emergency and non-emergency calls. Provided, however, that in the

maintenance, individual study, and response to all emergency and non-emergency calls.

3. Specialty Teams. A Specialty Team performs a level of service beyond EMS and fire
suppression to the citizens of Boise. The determination to provide a level of service is
a management right. Should BOISE CITY determine it will eliminate a Specialty Team,
the Specialty Team members will not be required to perform on a Specialty Team and
Specialty Team Pay will terminate at the end of the bi-week that the Specialty Team
is eliminated. Eligibility to receive appointment to a Specialty Team shall include:
a. Longevity – Completion of initial hire probationary period, and
b. Request to Join – Written or email request to Special Operations Division
Chief (waived if draft required), and
c. May not exceed two (2) Specialty Teams, and
d. Competitive test and/or minimum skills test at the discretion of the Special
Operations Division Chief.
Specialty Team Pay will begin the first day of the bi-week following appointment.
Specialty Team Longevity Pay will begin on the first day of the bi-week in which the
appointment anniversary date falls. Specialty Team Longevity Pay must be for continuous
service, and if there is a break in service, previous time will not count. Members may be
on a maximum of two (2) Specialty Teams, provided it is approved by the Special
Operations Division Chief.

4. Paramedic-Fire Fighters.
a. Definition: Members who are certified by the State of Idaho as an EMTParamedic; approved by the Fire Department of BOISE CITY and Ada
County/City Emergency Services System (ACCESS) Medical Director to
function as a Paramedic, and are assigned by the Operations/EMS Division
Chief to serve as a Paramedic-Fire Fighter.
b. Condition of Employment: Candidates for Paramedic-Fire Fighter positions with
the Fire Department of BOISE CITY shall serve as a Paramedic for as long as
the Operations/EMS Division Chief deems that it is in the best interest of the
organization. A Paramedic-Fire Fighter may submit a request to revert back to
EMT-B status. The Paramedic-Fire Fighter may be allowed to revert back to
EMT-B at the full discretion of the Operations/EMS Division Chief.
c. Training: BOISE CITY will provide or make available the training classes
necessary for re-licensure. BOISE CITY shall not be restricted from scheduling
such training by any limitations of Article 5. HOURS OF WORK; Section F.
Work Duties. Regular Duty Trades by Paramedic-Fire Fighters that will
negatively impact the staffing of the designated engine company or will cause
a Paramedic-Fire Fighter to miss scheduled required paramedic Continuing
Education Units (CEUs) must be approved by the Battalion Chief.
d. Overtime: Staffing requirements for Paramedic Engine Companies shall take
precedence in the distribution of overtime over the contractual reference to
distributing overtime as equally as practicable among members of the same
rank.

participate on one (1) Specialty Team, unless otherwise approved by the Fire
Chief. Paramedic-Fire Fighters not participating on a Specialty Team will be
eligible to be drafted to a Specialty Team.
