ORDINANCE 4__

AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE FOR THE BUDGET OF THE CITY OF TEGA CAY
GENERAL FUND FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING OCTOBER 1, 2014, AND
ENDING SEPTEMBER 30, 2015, TO PROVIDE FOR THE RECEIPT AND EXPENDITURE
OF FUNDS, AND TO PROVIDE FOR THE RECEIPT AND EXPENDITURE OF SPECIAL
PROJECT FUNDS, AND OTHER MATTERS RELATED HERETO.
BE IT ORDAINED by the Mayor and City Council of the City of Tega Cay duly assembled:
SECTION 1 - Annual Budget. For the purposes of providing for the orderly administration of government
in the City of Tega Cay, there is hereby enacted an annual budget for all revenues for the City of Tega Cay, for
the Fiscal Year 2014 -2015, commencing October 1, 2014, and ending September 30, 2015. The Annual Budget
for Fiscal Year 2014- 2015 is appended hereto and made a part of this Ordinance.

SECTION 2 - General Fund Revenue. In order to provide services to the citizens of Tega Cay and to
provide for the public health and welfare of our community, the Tega Cay FY 2014 - 2015 anticipates and
forecasts the following revenue and revenue sources for the City's General Fund:
General Property Tax

$4,280,409

SECTION 3 - Charges and Fees. Pursuant to South Carolina Code, the City of Tega Cay will continue to
assess service charges and fees necessary to support the operations and development needs as prescribed by City
Council and City staff. Such service charges and fees are included on the attached Exhibits A for Operations
Division, Development Services Division, Parks & Recreation Fees, Facility Rentals and Police Department.

SECTION 4 - Levy of Taxes. For the purposes of generating revenue for the provision of City services there
shall be set, the millage rate of eighty-nine (89) mills. Said millage will be levied against all property located
within the City limits of the municipality unless otherwise exempt from taxation pursuant to Title XII, South
Carolina Code of Laws, 1976 as amended. The tax shall be collected pursuant to the contractual arrangements
with the Treasurers of York County in the manner as provided for the collection of municipal taxes by Title XII,
Chapter 51 of the South Carolina Code of Laws, 1976, as amended. The Treasurer's books shall open on October
1, 2014, or as soon thereafter as practical, for the payment of such taxes and shall close on January 15, 2015, at
which time taxes due and payable which have not been paid shall become delinquent. City Council hereby adopts
York County's policies for the collection and imposition of interest, penalties, and costs regarding City property
tax collections.

SECTION 5 - Collection of Motor Vehicle Taxes. Taxes levied on motor vehicles may be collected
pursuant to the contractual arrangements with the Treasurers of York County. Such taxes shall be paid on the
schedule as established by State Statute and nothing herein shall be deemed to extend or defer the time of payment
for such motor vehicle taxes.

Page 1 of 4

SECTION 6 - Owner of Motor Vehicles. No owner of a motor vehicle registered in the State of South
Carolina and property of a person, a resident of the City, shall be operated on the streets and public ways in the
City unless all motor vehicle taxes duly assessed against such vehicle shall have first been paid. In the event that
a person violates the provisions of this Section, he/she shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and subject to the
penalties prescribed in Title 46, South Carolina Code of Laws, 1997 as amended. Nothing in this Section shall
preclude the collection of taxes upon such motor vehicle after the prosecution of the offender for failure to pay
such taxes.

SECTION 7 - Receipt of Funds.

The City Finance Director, or his/her designee, shall deposit General
Account Funds paid to the account of the City in such bank accounts as deemed advisable. Funds received by
any other official or employee of the City shall be paid over daily to the Finance Director, or his/her designee.
The Finance Director, or his/her designee, shall establish an accounting system which shall ensure the proper
segregation of funds prescribed by this Ordinance and further shall segregate all funds received from special
projects, state or federal programs, as required, so that such funds are not co-mingled with funds received
pursuant to this Ordinance for the general operation of the City.

SECTION 8 - Accounting. The City Staff is directed to maintain special projects, state or federal programs in
a separate and segregated account, which may be transferred to the General Fund as needed. The City Staff shall
adhere to the regulations of the United States Department of the Treasury with respect to the administration and
expenditure of such funds.

SECTION 9 - Distribution of Funds. All funds shall be disbursed in accordance with the procurement
procedures of the City of Tega Cay and in accordance with pertinent federal and state laws and regulations.

SECTION 10 - Budget Year and Closing of Accounts. The budget year shall expire on September 30,
2015. No funds shall be disbursed pursuant to this Ordinance unless such funds have been obligated prior to the
close of the budget year. Within the meaning of this Section, the term "obligated" shall be deemed to include the
placing of an order or the signing of a contract for the delivery of goods or services. In the event that funds are
obligated during the budget year, they must be actually expended and disbursed within sixty (60) days from the
closing of the budget year.

of the City's various accounts by resolution. Pursuant to the South Carolina Code of Law, the City Council
