Item Title: Discuss and Consider approval of the Meet and Confer Agreement between the Forney
Professional Fire Association and the City of Forney. (Staff recommends approval of the item.)
Summary Statement
Meet & Confer is a permissive process allowed and prescribed by the Texas Local Government Code
(TLGC) Chapter 142. The process provides an opportunity to supersede, modify, and improve upon the
statutory requirements of Civil Service law.
We are requesting approval of the execution of the Meet and Confer Agreement Between the City of
Forney, Texas, and the Forney Professional Firefighters Association to Expire on September 30, 2024.
The agreement covers the following items:
• Association Business Leave
• Compensation
• Fire Marshal’s Office On-Call Pay
• Fitness Incentive Program
• Hiring/Recruitment
• Promotional Process
• Rank Structure
• Sick Leave Payout
• Supplemental Pays
