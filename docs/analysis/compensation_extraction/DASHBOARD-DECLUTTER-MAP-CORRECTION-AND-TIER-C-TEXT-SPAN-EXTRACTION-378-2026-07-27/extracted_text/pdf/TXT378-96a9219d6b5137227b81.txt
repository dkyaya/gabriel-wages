Delran Township Budget Process
 The 2020 budget process began in October of 2019 with the department heads meeting with
administration to examine the costs and processes necessary to deliver the service needs of
the community.
 This culminated in the Mayor submitting his budget to the Township Council on February 25th
2020 for their review.
 Township Council reviewed the budget at work sessions through the month of March and, due
to the declared emergency in regard to COVID-19, the State of New Jersey extended the
budget deadlines.
 The Township Council introduced the budget on May 5, 2020 and scheduled a public hearing
on June 2, 2020.
 The resulting municipal budget calls for no tax increase and no sewer rate increase.

2020 Municipal Budget
 The budget calls for no increase in taxes!
 Appropriations - less than 2019
 Spending Plan - below the state cap on appropriations
 Tax Levy Cap - below the state cap on taxes
 Sewer Budget No Rate Increase (10th year in a row)

Delran Net Valuation

 Delran has a Net Valuation Taxable of $1,425,281,690.
 This is the total valuation of all properties within the Township of Delran
that are subject to property taxes.
 Each penny of the tax rate yields $142,528.

4

The Big Picture
Tax Rate Impact on Tax Payers

 “What is the impact of an increase or decrease on the average residential tax
payer?”
 From the Tax Collectors book there are 5,170 homes in Delran with a total
valuation of $1,095,433,100 for an average assessment of $211,882 per
home in Delran.
 Based upon this information each penny on the tax rate represent an average
homeowner $21.18 per year, since the tax rate is per thousand of the
assessment.
 This Municipal Budget calls for no tax increase!

5

Delran Municipal Tax rate in 2020
Utilizing this years tax rate and this years average assessment the average home
in Delran pays $1,631 for the municipal tax rate and $48.60 for the open space
tax.
These are the only funds that the Township of Delran keeps from the property tax
bill. The remaining portions of your property tax bill is determined by and paid to
the School, the County and the Fire District.
This municipal budget calls for no tax increase. So, the rate will remain the same.

 Remember: The Municipal Rate represents just 21.9 percent of the total
