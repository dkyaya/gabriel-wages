Ridley Township Board of Commissioners
May 28, 2025
Meeting Minutes
The Regular Meeting of the Ridley Township Board of Commissioners was called to order
by Board President Willert at 6:00pm on May 28, 2025 at the Township Building.
The following Commissioners were present: Mr. Saraceni, Mrs. Morrisette (on phone), Mrs.
Cummins, Mr. McMenamin, Mr. Saunders, Mr. Warwick, Mr. Willert, Mr. Capozzoli and Mr.
Bidoli.
Also present were Mr. Ryan (Township Manager), Mr. Catania (Township Engineer), Mr.
Betzler (Controller) and Mr. Neill (Township Solicitor).
The Pledge of Allegiance was recited.
Public Forum
- Kim Frick – 1276 Woodcrest Terrace, appeared before the Board concerning parking
issues on the street, speeding along Fairview Road and posting of meeting minutes.
-Suzanne de Seife – 1209 Harper Avenue, appeared before the Board concerning pedestrian
safety updates.
- Doug Hocker – 1209 Harper Avenue, appeared before the Board concerning pedestrian
safety issues, State Police speeding enforcement along MacDade Boulevard and pedestrian
safety education in the schools.
Executive Session Announcement
Mr. Willert announced the Board met in an Executive Session on May 28th to discuss
personnel matters.
Anne E. Howanski Memorial Scholarship
Mr. Willert announced Andrew Shay as this year’s recipient of the Anne E. Howanski
Memorial Scholarship at Ridley High School
Delco Gymnastics
Mr. Willert congratulated the Delco Gymnastics travel team on their accomplishments in
the USIGC PA State Championships.

Manager’s Report
Mr. Ryan requested approval of two (2) Block Party Requests – Sixth & Folsom Avenues on
June 14th and Mole Road on June 22nd.
Motion made by Mrs. Cummins, seconded by Mr. Bidoli to approve the block parties
as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Ryan requested approval of a Conditional Use application from TVI/Savers for the
property at 2600 MacDade Blvd. subject to the conditions set forth by the Ridley Township
Planning Commission.
Motion made by Mrs. Morrisette, seconded by Mr. Warwick to approve the
Conditional Use application as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
Mr. Ryan requested approval to ratify the Delaware County FOP Lodge 27 and Ridley
Township Police collective bargaining agreement which calls for a 12.75% wage increase
over 3 years along with residency requirements and medical benefit modifications.
Motion made by Mr. Warwick, seconded by Mrs. Cummins to ratify the Police
contract as presented. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).

Library & Resource Center Committee Report

downloaded 14,960 books and other items in April. Program attendance at 80 programs
was 2,105 people. In addition, the library staff accepted 163 new passport applications in
April, bringing the total for 2025 to 629. She also reported on upcoming library programs.

subject to the Controller’s review and approval. Motion carried unanimously (9-0).
