12. Town Council Comments
● Rodney Alexander: We need to look at what Hartford Healthcare is doing about the maternity
ward or birthing center.
● Dawn Niles: is concerned about the closure of the walk-in clinic. The closest walk-in clinic is
at UCONN. What is going on and how do we get one back? Thursday the 23rd Madeline is
turning 100. Going to take her some proclamations and celebrate at 11am, the Council is
invited.

● Joe Pliss: heard at the WDNA meeting the challenges of getting a business up and running.
The health department is one of them. The town helps a lot, but there seems to be a disconnect
with businesses. Would like for Windham to be one of the easiest towns in CT to do business.
● Rose Reyes: Windham United to Save our Healthcare will be on the radio in June, and they
will be asking about the status of the Hartford Healthcare feasibility study for a birthing center
. We got 146 baby bombs. The Willimantic Taxing district didn’t get a chance to review the
police and fire contracts, was that an oversight? A new CSO officer was approved by the
Willimantic Taxing District for $80,000 to alleviate the overtime burden? I have a connection
with the New Haven director of the Fair Rent Commission. She is willing to provide us with
application templates and training. Encouraged someone from the public school system to
connect with Economic Development so we can work on a marketing strategy to recruit
educators. Why are 480 and 560 buildings not on the blight report? She would like to see
enterprise and zoning review at the Willimantic Taxing District.
● Tony Fantoli: After the Memorial Day Pilgrimage and Parade, there will be hot dogs and
hamburgers at the American Legion. He would like to see more people show up and show
their appreciation at the Memorial Day events.
● Ernie Eldridge: Jillson House will be named as a hospitality center for the Ride the East bike
ride on June 8th.
