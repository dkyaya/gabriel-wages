ORDINANCE NO. O-05-2024
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE “PERSONNEL AND
PAY CLASSIFICATION PLAN” BY ADDING NON-UNION
POLICE DEPARTMENT POSITIONS AND PAY RANGES,
EFFECTIVE ON SEPTEMBER 1, 2024.
WHEREAS, council had determined that addition of non-union police department positions to
the “Personnel and Pay Classification Plan” is necessary and appropriate.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF FOR THOMAS, CAMPBELL COUNTY, KENTUCKY, AS FOLLOWS:
SECTION I
The City of Fort Thomas hereby updates the Pay and Classification Plan to include nonunion Police Department positions and pay to be effective on September 1, 2024.
SECTION II
The City of Fort Thomas hereby amends and readopts the compensation ranges for various
authorized positions with the Personnel and Pay Classification Plan as identified in “Exhibit B”
which is attached hereto, to be effective on September 1, 2024. Additionally, members of Police
Department and General Government full-time, non-union employees shall not receive pay raises
that are less than any other department within the city that have bargaining agents negotiating
compensation with city leadership.
SECTION III
All ordinances, and/or parts thereof, in conflict with the provisions of this ordinance, are
to the extent of such conflict, hereby repealed.
SECTION IV
This ordinance shall be effective when read, adopted, and advertised according to law.
