PREAMBLE
THIS AGREEMENT is entered into effective the first (1st) day of July, 2023, between the City
of Jenks, Oklahoma, hereinafter referred to as "Employer" and Local 2567, International
Association of Fire Fighters, hereinafter referred to as "Union", to provide for the equitable and
peaceful adjustment of differences which may arise, and to provide proper standards of wages and
hours and other conditions of employment and to encourage more effective service in the public
interest.
RECOGNITION
The Employer recognizes the Union as the sole and exclusive Bargaining Agent for all
permanent paid members of the Fire Department, excluding the Chief of the Department and an
Administrative Assistant. For the purpose of this Agreement the position of Administrative Assistant
shall be determined by the Employer.
The Union recognizes the City Manager, or his designated representative, as the sole
representative of the City of Jenks for the purpose of collective bargaining. The Union and Employer
agree to bargain in good faith on all matters relating to wages, hours, and other conditions of
employment.
The spirit and intent of this Agreement shall apply to all probationary employees during their
initial twelve (12) month period of employment; however, probationary employees will not be
accorded access to the provisions of this Agreement in cases of grievance or disciplinary action,
including termination, but shall have access to grievance procedures of the City of Jenks Manual
for Personnel Policies, Procedures, Classifications and Remuneration.
ARTICLE 1
UNION BUSINESS
SECTION 1. The Employer will continue the practice of dues deductions during the term of
this Agreement. It is further understood that union business shall not be conducted on the Employer's
premises during regular work hours except to investigate grievances, attend negotiation meetings
and/or labor/management meetings. The above activities will be permitted provided there are no
uncompleted duties or work and provided it does not interfere with the operations of the Fire
Department.
SECTION 2. The Union shall have the right to use the Fire Station to hold Union meetings.
SECTION 3. The Union shall be granted time not to exceed four (4) shifts with pay per year to
perform Union functions, including attendance at conventions, conferences and seminars with pay. Two
(2) additional shifts shall be granted annually for attendance at the I.A.F.F. Conventions to be held in
August of each year. This time shall not interfere with the normal operations of the Fire Department.
Members of the negotiation team shall be allowed time off, if needed, for all meetings which shall be
mutually set by the Employer and the Union.
SECTION 4. Requests for time off must be given to the Chief or Assistant Chief at least
seven (7) days prior to the Union event.

ARTICLE 2
GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
SECTION 1. The Union or any employee covered under this Agreement prior to filing a
grievance may submit a concern to the Operations, Health, and Safety Committee for resolution of
the concern in an attempt to forego a formal grievance. This process shall not start grievance time
limitations until the Committee has met to consider the concern. The Committee shall consist of two
representatives of the Union, the Assistant Fire Chief, and a member of central City management.
The Committee shall meet within five (5) business days of Union or management notification. The
Committee shall make recommendation, within three (3) business days, to the Fire Chief. The Fire
Chief will have three (3) business days to affirm, modify, or reject the recommendation.
SECTION 2: The Union or any employee covered under this Agreement may file a grievance
within ten (10) business days of the alleged occurrence, as hereinafter defined, and shall be afforded
the full protection of this Agreement.
SECTION 3. The Union President, or his authorized representative may report an
impending grievance to the Fire Chief in an effort to forestall its occurrence.
SECTION 4. Any controversy between the Employer and the Union or any employee
concerning the interpretation, enforcement or application of any provision of this Agreement,
concerning any of the terms or conditions of employment contained in this Agreement:
A.

The grievance shall be discussed by the employee with the Fire Chief, either by
the employee or his designee. A firefighter who is available at the time, shall be
present at said discussion. The answer may be submitted orally by the Fire Chief
within ten (10) business days to the employee involved or to the Union President.

B.

If the grievance is not settled by the provision of Section 4A, it shall be submitted
in writing to the Fire Chief within ten (10) business working days. The Chief shall
submit his answer in writing to the employee involved, or the bargaining agent
within ten (10) business days. If the grievance has not been settled within that
time, it then shall be sent to the City Manager within ten (10) business days.

C.

The City Manager shall submit his answer in writing to the Fire Chief, the employee
involved, and an officer of the Union within ten (10) business days. If the City Manager
and the Union Grievance Committee have not settled the grievance within that time,
it shall be submitted to mediation for adjustment as follows:
1.

Within ten (10) business days, the Union shall request mediation and the
parties shall agree to a third-party mediator according to one of two (2)
methods: (a) The parties will mutually agree on a third-party mediator and any
cost associated with the same shall be borne equally by the parties; or (b)
The Union shall request the services of a mediator from the Federal Mediation
and Conciliation Service.

2.

If the matter submitted to mediation is not resolved, the Union shall advise
the City Manager of its decision to arbitrate, in writing, within ten (10) days

5

of the conclusion of mediation and shall request a panel of arbitrators pursuant
to Section 4, C3 of this Article within thirty (30) days of such notice.
3.

The party declaring impasse shall request a panel of seven (7) arbitrators from

opportunity of promotions.

compensatory leave or be granted a leave of absence without pay for the length

B.

Any fees received for federal or state witness or jury service may be subtracted from
the pay of any employee on court leave.

C.

An employee serving such duty shall present to his department head the original
summons or subpoena from the court prior to being excused from duty and at the
conclusion of the duty, a signed statement from the clerk of the court showing the
actual dates of attendance at the court.

D.

action shall be compensated by the Employer at time and one-half (1/2). This shall not

of receipt, he will be granted one-half (1/2) shift off the day immediately before the
scheduled jury duty if he is on duty and will not be expected to return to work until
released from the balance of his actual jury duty, and then shall return to work within
two hours if at least one-third (1/3) of a shift remains.

ARTICLE 42
FIRE MARSHAL
SECTION 1. Operations to Fire Marshal Transfers
A. These transfers will only be made when there is an opening in the Fire Marshal assignment. The
promotional procedure outlined in the departments Standard Operating Guidelines will be followed
to fill the Fire Marshal Position.
B. When a transfer from Operations to Fire Marshal is made, the Operations rank of the individual
will be maintained. This will not be an active rank but shall be used if the individual goes back to an
Operations position. If the individual returns to Operations duty, he/she would return either to the
rank held prior to moving into the Fire Marshal position, or if that rank is unavailable the individual
may move to a lesser rank. The individual recognizes and agrees to the lesser pay for such lesser
rank. However, the individual will maintain seniority.
SECTION 2. Fire Marshal to Operations Transfers
A. If an opening in Operations occurs for reasons including a retirement, resignation, termination,
promotion or staffing increase, an Administrative Officer may request that he/she return to
Operations and fill the open position returning either to the rank held prior to moving into the Fire
Marshal position, or if that rank is unavailable the individual may move to a lesser rank. If to a lesser
rank, the individual recognizes and agrees to the lesser pay for such lesser rank. However, the
individual will maintain seniority.
B. If there is not an existing opening in Operations, the Fire Marshal will make his/her desire known
to return to his former Operations position known to the Chief. The Chief shall post the potential
availability of the Fire Marshal position to ascertain whether there is any member interested in the
position.
C. If an external candidate is selected for the Fire Marshal’s position, there will be no option for Fire
Marshal to Operations transfer.
SECTION 3. Work Period, Overtime, Stand-By
A. The work period for the Fire Marshal shall be fourteen (14) days, eighty (80) hours in a two-week
pay period.
B. The Fire Marshall shall earn overtime at a rate of not less than one and one-half (1 ½) times the
regular hourly rate of pay, or compensatory time off at a rate of one and one-half (1 ½) times the
hours worked, for all hours worked over eighty (80) in any work period. Overtime compensated by

A. Should a current member transfer from Operations to the Fire Marshal, all accrued vacation
leave at the time of the transfer shall be converted at a shift to shift basis (e.g., if employee has 20

approve or disapprove any paid vacation request.
E. Upon City's receipt of a letter of resignation or termination, the Fire Marshal shall be
compensated for the balance of all accrued vacation at the Fire Marshal's regular hourly rate of pay
so long as the employee has worked at least 12 months for the City of Jenks.

The Fire Marshal shall not be regularly scheduled to work on any paid holiday recognized by the
City. Paid holidays and floating holidays shall be paid as per City policy.

Re:

Agreement for Work on Oklahoma Turnpike Authority Property

City staff met with the Secretary of Transportation and representatives of the Oklahoma
Turnpike Authority (OT A) on July 18, 2023 to discuss various topics. One topic was requested
by the Simon Premium Outlet construction team relating to aesthetics of the Turnpike property
on the north side of the mall. There were three (3) items discussed, including:
1 - Removal of brush and trees along the fence line in order to clean up the appearance of
the property along the north side of the mall property (see the attached pictures);
2 - Repaint the box creating access from the service road on the north and south side of
the Turnpike under the turnpike (see attached pictures); and
3 - Installation of lighting inside the access box.
In addition, the City requested consideration for removal of the fencing on both sides of the
Turnpike to allow for easier mowing, less ability for debris accumulation, and a cleaner overall
appearance. OTA expressed some concern that vehicles may be able to traverse the Turnpike
side slopes without having the fencing. Since City roads on both sides of the Turnpike are
primarily curb and gutter, it was agreed that the fences could be eliminated.
To be consistent system-wide, OTA requested the City enter an "Agreement for Work on OTA
Property" detailing responsibilities for the above items (see the attached Agreement). Although
the Outlet Mall will maintain the property adjacent to their development and make improvements
to the access box, the City is intended to be the liaison for this work.
Staff recommends approval of this Agreement between the Oklahoma Turnpike Authority
(OTA) and the City of Jenks for the ability to work on OTA property from Elm Street east to the
Arkansas River to include removing brush and trees along the fence line in the vicinity of the
Simon Premium Outlet, maintaining the surface of the Access Box, installation of lighting in the
Access Box, and mowing from inside the service road curb to 2 feet past the fence line to be
removed by OTA.
