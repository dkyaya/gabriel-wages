AWARD
Upon presentation of the parties’ evidence and arguments, it is hereby found
and ordered the parties successor collective bargaining agreement to the May 1, 2021
– April 30, 2025 contract shall be as follows:
1.

Duration:
Four years: May 1, 2025 - April 30, 2029

2.

Wages:
Effective May 1, 2025: 3.75% increase
Effective May 1, 2026: 3.75% increase
Effective May 1, 2027: 3.75% increase
Effective May 1, 2028: 3.75% increase

3.

Vacation:
40 hours of vacation will be granted as a lump sum upon
an officer’s hire and the newly hired officer will also begin

Village of Algonquin and MAP Chapter 78
Page 2
to accrue vacation at two hours per pay period so that at
the end of one year, that officer will have 48 hours of vacation.
4.

5.

Retroactivity:
A.

Except as specified below, wages as provided in paragraph 2 are retroactive to May 1, 2025 on all hours
worked for employees who are currently employed
as of the date of this Award.

B.

Vacations as provided in paragraph 3 are retroactive
for all employees who were hired on or after April 1,
2025 and are currently employed as of the date of
this Award.

C.

Employees who retired since May 1, 2025 are eligible for the retroactive wages under this Award.

D.

Employees who were terminated are not entitled to
retroactive benefits under this Award.

E.

Employees who voluntarily left employment with
the Village (aside from retiring) are not entitled to
retroactive benefits under this Award.

Prior Tentative Agreements:
Prior tentative agreements reached by the parties are
adopted into the terms of this Award.

6.

Other Proposals:
Other proposals not addressed in this Award are dropped.

7.

Remand for Drafting:
This matter is now remanded to the parties for drafting of
language consistent with the terms of this Award.
