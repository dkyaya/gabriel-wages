Selection of Benchmark Positions
• Benchmark positions are those jobs that are commonly found at most
of the competitor employers
• No such thing as a “perfect match”
• Benchmarks are defined as 70% similar duties, responsibilities, and
Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities (KSAs)
• Data collected for 80 current positions and 10 future positions

10

Application of Survey Data
• Midpoint-to-Midpoint analysis – the traditional
method of analysis for public sector surveys
• Use the midpoint as our point of comparison
because it is the most objective, consistent
component of salary structures; comparator
agencies have salary structures with varying range
width
• Midpoint theoretically represents value for journeylevel employee performing job duties and
responsibilities with proficiency
• Do not prefer to compare actual salaries as that can
be affected by longevity, cert. pays, other factors

Internal Equity/Internal Alignment
• Use survey data as primary guide for range placement.
• Also consider how positions relate to one another within the organization,
which positions the organization values similarly.
• As responsibilities increase, so should the range.
• General Rule of Thumb:
• At least 10-15% (2-3 ranges) between sequential job series.
• At least 20-25% (4-5 ranges) between supervisors and direct reports.

• Encourage career progression and progression within job families/series.
• Additional levels and positions surveyed and modeled out for future
reference

Implementation Scenario: Option 1
• Adopt proposed range assignments
• Adjust sworn police salary schedule by 5%
• Move all employees to the range minimum
• Estimated Cost: $71,842 or .82% of current payroll

19

Implementation Scenario: Option 2
• Adopt proposed range assignments
• Adjust sworn police salary schedule by 5%
• Move all employees to the step that is closest to, but not
less than, their current salary in the proposed range
• Estimated Cost: $167,040, or 1.92% of current payroll

20

Implementation Scenario: Option 3
• Adopt proposed range assignments
• Adjust sworn police salary schedule by 5%
• Move all employees to the step that is closest to, but not less than, their
current salary in the proposed range
• Provide all employees with a step increase
• Estimated Cost: $389,238, or 4.47% of current payroll

21

Implementation Scenario: Option 4
• Adopt proposed range assignments
• Adjust sworn police salary schedule by 5%
• Employees retain their current step assignment in the proposed range
• Estimated Cost: $660,223, or 7.58% of current payroll
