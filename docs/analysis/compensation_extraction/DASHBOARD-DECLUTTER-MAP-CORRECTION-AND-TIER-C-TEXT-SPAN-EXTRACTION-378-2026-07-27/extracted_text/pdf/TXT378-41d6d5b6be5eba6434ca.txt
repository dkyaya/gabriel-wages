PUBLIC HEARING AND VOTING ON LIQUOR LICENSE TRANSFER
1. RESOLUTION NO. 2961
TITLE: A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE TRANSFER OF PENNSYLVANIA
RESTAURANT LIQUOR LICENSE NUMBER R-16136 FROM THE
PENNSYLVANIA LIQUOR CONTROL BOARD TO WEIS MARKETS, INC.,
1000 S. SECOND STREET, SUNBURY, PA, FOR USE AT 3644 MACARTHUR
ROAD, WHITEHALL TOWNSHIP, LEHIGH COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA.
(BOC)
President ARMSTRONG read Resolution No. 2961 to the Board.
Commissioner GINDER moved to approve, seconded by Commissioner MARX.
Attorney Ellen FREEMAN, of O’Hara and Flaherty representing Weis Markets, stated the
license would be used for a restaurant within the current grocery store with seating for 30
patrons, a selection of prepared food to consume on premise or to grab-and-go, will sell beer
for on premise consumption and to go along with wine. President ARMSTRONG asked
where it would be located in the store. Attorney FREEMAN stated the front left of the
building, hours of operation would be Monday thru Saturday 8:00 am to midnight and
Sunday from 9:00 am to 11:00 pm. There will be a dedicated restaurant manager who will
receive RAMP training along with all servers, will offer on premise single servings of beer
with a limit of 2 beers, also take out of 2 six packs and 4 bottles of wine, all purchases must
be made at the café register and there will be no type of entertainment. Commissioner DUTT
asked if there will be a sign stating drinks cannot be taken out of a certain area and how will
it be enforced. Attorney FREEMAN stated there will be signage and a delineation of 4 foot
bollards between the café and the grocery store.
David GILL, Weis Markets Director of Architecture, stated it would be completely delineated
with the bollards, different flooring, would be located where the Pharmacy is now which will
be relocated. Commissioner SNYDER asked if all the conditions are stated in this resolution,
would like to see the hours of operation stated. Mr. GILL noted the hours are controlled by
the Liquor Control Board, they follow their laws. Attorney FREEMAN stated they would
have all of the privileges of a restaurant liquor license, will not be operating as a bar, will put
the hours in the resolution but reserve the right to come back before the Board if they want to
change the hours.
Kerry KRAMER, 1259 Echo Drive, Whitehall, asked how the police plan on enforcing this
with people coming out onto MacArthur Road. Chief MARKS stated they would not do
anything different than what they do with other restaurants or bars or anything else in the
Township business wise, will treat everyone the same and if there is a violation of the law,
will deal with it.
Seven Commissioners were present and voted “yes”. Resolution No. 2961 was approved
with the added conditions as presented.

WHITEHALL TOWNSHIP, TO PROVIDE ACCESS TO THE PROPERTY OF
THOMAS L. BARTHOLOMEW AT 925 FRONT STREET, WHITEHALL,

President ARMSTRONG read Resolution No. 2967 to the Board.
Commissioner GINDER moved to approve, seconded by Commissioner MARX. There

approved.

REPORTS OF PUBLIC OFFICIALS
1.

Commissioner SNYDER welcomed Boy Scout Conner ZAPE of Troop 59 who was in
attendance.

2.

Commissioner MARX thanked the Mayor and Mrs. SWEENEY for getting the info on
the Veteran’s Memorial dedication in the next newsletter which will be on Memorial
Day, May 29th at 1:00 p.m.

3.

Commissioner DUTT stated on recent trip to CA was given a tour by the firefighters of
the Fire Station from the 70’s show Emergency, asked our Fire Chief to send a
Whitehall Fire Department patch to Culver City where it is now on display within that
Fire Station. He is very proud of the Whitehall Senior Center Committee, the
dedication and grand opening is Tuesday, April 4, 2017 at 10:00 am at the West Catty
Playground Recreation Building. The Chamber is running it, there will be
refreshments and sign-ups for the different activities to start on May 2nd and if anyone
needs transportation, to contact the Area Agency on Aging to sign up for Easton
Coach.

4.

President ARMSTRONG reminded residents Saturday, April 22nd is the MacArthur
75th Gala at the Whitehall Mall, tickets are available, hoping everyone comes out to
celebrate the 75th Anniversary of the first six lane road in a town in the State of
Pennsylvania, another Whitehall pride.

5.

Mayor HOZZA thanked the Public Works Department and everyone involved during
our first snow storm of the year, everyone performed admirably which was key
because we have a lot of new people who had to be trained. He stated the launch of
the refuse carts is completed, went very well, thanked everyone for their patience and
understanding, noted requests for second carts is only open from January until the first
week of February because of billing, those requesting it pay a $100 deposit and their
garbage fee stays at the prior rate of $330 while the fee for 2017 is $300.00 for
everyone else. Mayor HOZZA stating the Coplay Aggregates situation is fluid and
hoping to have answers from the New Jersey DEP on where the material is coming
from.
President ARMSTRONG stated they probably read in the papers about how Public
Officials are yelling and screaming and not getting anything done, hopes they are
proud of the Whitehall Board, we work together and whether we agree or disagree,
handle things well and do get things done.
