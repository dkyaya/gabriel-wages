FRANCONIA TOWNSHIP
REGULAR MINUTES
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
Chairman Grey Godshall called the Regular Meeting of the Board of Supervisors to order on
Monday, October 16, 2023, at 6:06 p.m. at the Municipal Building, 671 Allentown Road,
Telford, Pennsylvania 18969.
Present were Chairman Grey Godshall, Vice Chairman David B. Fazio, Supervisor Robert
Nice, Supervisor Charles Amuso and Supervisor Jill Halteman. Also, in attendance were
Township Manager Jon A. Hammer, Township Solicitor Eric Wert, Esquire, Township
Engineer Douglas C. Rossino P.E, and interested residents of Franconia Township.
Open meeting to the Public
Chairman Godshall opened the meeting to the public. No comments were heard.
Chairman Godshall announced that in the Work Session, the 2024 budget was reviewed and
there is no proposed tax increase.
APPROVAL OF THE SEPTEMBER 18, 2023 REGULAR MEETING MINUTES
Supervisor Fazio motioned for the approval of the minutes from the September 18, 2023
meeting. It was seconded by Supervisor Halteman, and all voted in favor, 5-0.
SOLICITOR ‘S REPORT – ERIC WERT, ESQUIRE
Ordinance 423-23 Solicitor Wert announced that the Ordinance is an amendment to the
Police Pension Ordinance. Specifically changes the term DROP participation period from 36
months to 60 months. As a result of the last Police Collective Bargaining Agreement this is a
follow up. Supervisor Amuso made the motion to approve the Ordinance. It was seconded by
Supervisor Fazio and the motion carried.
POLICE REPORT- CHIEF MICHAEL MARTIN
Chief Martin submitted the statistical report prior to the meeting. He took the opportunity to
thank the Highway Crew for their time to fix the fields following the Fall Fest. Chief added
that the department has sent its newer officers for various trainings.

HIGHWAY REPORT-ANTHONY FRYDLEWICZ
Mr. Frydlewicz submitted his report prior to the meeting.
ENGINEERING REPORT- DOUGLAS C. ROSSINO, P.E.
Mr. Rossino submitted his report prior to the meeting. He added that 869 Sunset Lane is
under construction and the annual MS4 report was submitted at the end of September as
required.
TREASURER’S REPORT
A motion was made by Supervisor Nice and seconded by Supervisor Fazio to approve the
Treasurer’s Report as presented, and to provide authorization for the Treasurer to pay all bills
for the respective funds as presented on Voucher No. 23-16-10 dated October 16, 2023, in
the amount of $977,757.66. Mr. Nice reported that there were checks for $520,000 for
pension payments, $100,000 to the Fire Companies and about $75,000 for the pole building.
The motion passed, 5-0.
PAYROLL
A motion was made by Supervisor Nice and seconded by Supervisor Fazio for the Treasurer
to be authorized to pay all employees during the pay period of October 16, 2023, through
November 19, 2023, according to the hours worked as listed on the Payroll Journal for the
aforesaid period. The motion carried unanimously.
SUBDIVISION AND LAND DEVELOPMENT
None
