Bid Title: Compensation and Classification Study
Category: Request for Proposal
Status: Closed
Description:
Request for Proposal
The City of Jacksonville Texas is seeking proposals from qualified consultants to conduct a compensation and classification study.  The request for proposal will be available beginning September 11, 2021.
A copy of the request for proposal packet is available on the City website at www.jacksonvilletx.org, in the office of the city secretary at 315 S. Ragsdale, Jacksonville, TX 75766 or via email upon request.
Interested firms must submit one copy of their proposal via email to the City at and , no later than 5:00 p.m. CST., on September 30, 2021. Interested parties are solely responsible for ensuring that proposals are delivered on time. Proposals received after the due date and time will not be considered. For additional information, please contact James Hubbard at (903) 339-3300.
