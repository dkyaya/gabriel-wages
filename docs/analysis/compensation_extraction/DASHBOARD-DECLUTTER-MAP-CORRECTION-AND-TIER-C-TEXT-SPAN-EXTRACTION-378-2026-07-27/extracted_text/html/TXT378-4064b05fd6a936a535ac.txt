OCEA REPRESENTATION POLICY
The Okaloosa County Education Association (OCEA) is the bargaining agent for all Okaloosa instructional personnel. OCEA does not enforce the provisions of the master contract for non-members. OCEA policy restricts its officers, staff and building representatives from providing services to non-members. Services will not be provided for job-related problems that occurred before the individual became a member. OCEA and its affiliates provide professional protection for the members of OCEA only.
