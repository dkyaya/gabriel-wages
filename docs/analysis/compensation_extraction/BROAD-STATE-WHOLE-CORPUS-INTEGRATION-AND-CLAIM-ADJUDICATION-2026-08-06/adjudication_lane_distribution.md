# Five-lane adjudication distribution

- adjudication_lane_001: CLAIM-C, UNSUP-01, UNSUP-05 — compensation-level and local-comparison claims (T+0m)
- adjudication_lane_002: CLAIM-B, CLAIM-G — growth, step, COLA, and across-board claims (T+1m)
- adjudication_lane_003: CLAIM-D — staffing, vacancy, overtime, recruitment, and retention claims (T+2m)
- adjudication_lane_004: CLAIM-A, CLAIM-E, CLAIM-F — implementation, retroactivity, non-base, budget/pay-plan, and institutional mechanism claims (T+3m)
- adjudication_lane_005: CLAIM-H, UNSUP-02, UNSUP-03, UNSUP-04, UNSUP-06 — global/generalization, unsupported, counterexample, limitation, recovery, and residual claims (T+4m)
