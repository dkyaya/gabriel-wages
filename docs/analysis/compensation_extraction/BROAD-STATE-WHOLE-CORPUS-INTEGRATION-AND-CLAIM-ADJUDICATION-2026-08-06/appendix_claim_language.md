# Technical appendix claim language

## CLAIM-A

In the reviewed corpus, formal bargaining, arbitration, and factfinding provide recurring institutional channels that can set or preserve safety-compensation terms; the evidence establishes mechanism operation, not an average wage effect. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-B

In reviewed unit-cycle records, step, seniority, rank, and COLA provisions create recurring scheduled wage-growth paths; step progression leans safety in the available sample, while across-board results are mixed and COLA cells are sparse. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-C

Reviewed safety-compensation records contain structured non-base channels—including overtime, holiday, longevity, premium, stipend, and allowance provisions—that can raise compensation beyond base pay; no complete total-compensation sum is estimated. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-D

Reviewed sources explicitly use recruitment, retention, comparator, vacancy, and staffing pressure to justify schedule changes or targeted premiums in some public-safety settings; the staffing evidence is descriptive, not causal or prevalence evidence. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-E

Reviewed records show that retroactive effective dates and implementation clauses can convert some bargaining delays into payable increases or back pay; adoption alone is not payment, and only retained evidence with a paid stage is described as paid. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-F

Reviewed ordinances, budgets, pay plans, and classification records show how compensation proposals can be formalized or constrained by fiscal institutions; these records do not establish that every proposal was implemented or paid. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-G

Both safety and non-safety units use bargaining, steps, COLAs, and pay plans. The available evidence is consistent with a reinforcing safety-pressure bundle in some settings, but local and growth results are mixed and do not establish a uniform safety advantage. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## CLAIM-H

The reviewed corpus supports a bounded account of mechanisms and directional pressures affecting safety compensation more strongly than it supports any global wage-gap, prevalence, or causal-effect estimate. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## UNSUP-01

A national safety wage-gap estimate is not supported by the current corpus. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## UNSUP-02

A national prevalence estimate for any compensation mechanism is not supported by the current corpus. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## UNSUP-03

A causal effect estimate for any compensation mechanism is not supported by the current design. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## UNSUP-04

No regression-based claim is supported because no regression-ready design passed the gate and no regression was run. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## UNSUP-05

A precise claim that safety wages grow a fixed percentage faster is not supported by the available matched evidence. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.

## UNSUP-06

The evidence does not establish that a documented mechanism caused an observed wage difference. Tier composition, unit definitions, counterexamples, conflicts, and unresolved linkage are reported in the technical tables.
