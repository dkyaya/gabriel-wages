# Claim boundaries

Allowed: a quantitative direct-text row and qualitative mechanism record occur in the same committed source-review/hash lineage, and this may support later bounded claim review. A linkage does not estimate a wage effect and does not prove causation. Forbidden: wage-gap, wage-level outcome comparison, regression, treatment-effect, population-prevalence, national, or final causal claims. Values remain unnormalized, unimputed, and unannualized.
