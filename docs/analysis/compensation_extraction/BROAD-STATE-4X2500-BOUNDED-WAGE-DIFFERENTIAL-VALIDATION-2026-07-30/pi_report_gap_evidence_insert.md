# PI-report insert: bounded local documentary wage differences

Alburtis Ordinance No. 546 provides bounded local documentary evidence that, during the overlapping January-June 2018 window, the Chief of Police rate was $33.57 per hour and the Administrative Assistant rate was $11.22 per hour. On the current normalized basis, the arithmetic difference is $22.35 per hour, or 199.2% relative to the administrative-assistant rate. This is a current source-grounded comparison of two municipal position rates—not a final wage-gap estimate or a matched bargaining-unit comparison—and it is not nationally representative; the Chief's exclusion from the bargaining unit and unresolved role, schedule, hours, and position equivalence require manual/legal/substantive validation before analytic use.

On the current normalized hourly basis, the listed safety-side maximum is $5.00 per hour higher, or 25.0% above the listed non-safety maximum. This is bounded local documentary evidence about maximum authorized schedule rates, not confirmed actual pay; Section 2 permits actual salaries below those maxima, and the packet copy's blank adoption/approval fields require manual or legal confirmation before analytic use. It is not a final wage-gap estimate, nationally representative conclusion, prevalence statement, policy-effect claim, or causal finding.

In Canastota, New York, bounded local documentary evidence from the Village's 2023-2024 salary schedule pairs the Police Officer Year 1 step for officers hired after 1999 at $23.91 per hour with the Code Enforcement Officer rate at $24.82 per hour. On the current normalized basis, the police entry-step value is $0.91 per hour lower, or 3.67% below the code-enforcement value. This current source-grounded comparison is a matched municipality-cycle candidate, not a final wage-gap estimate and not nationally representative; it requires manual and substantive validation of tenure, experience, duties, hours, and schedule-position equivalence before analytic use.
