# Candidate caveats

## Alburtis, PA

Same ordinance and overlapping period, but not matched bargaining-unit contracts: the Chief was outside the police bargaining unit, and role/rank/hours/schedule equivalence with the Administrative Assistant is unverified. Use only as bounded local documentary evidence after manual/legal/substantive validation, not as a final wage-gap estimate.

## Cammack Village, AR

Same-page hourly maximum schedule values and arithmetic are validated, but Section 2 permits actual salaries below the maxima and the council-packet copy has blank adoption/approval fields; use only as a bounded local comparison of listed maximum rates pending manual/legal validation.

## Canastota, NY

The police value is specifically the Year 1 entry step for officers hired after 1999, while the Code Enforcement Officer line provides a single listed rate with no tenure, step, or experience label. The source establishes common municipality, cycle, page, hourly pay basis, and schedule-listed wage status, but it does not establish comparable tenure, experience, duties, qualifications, hours, full-time status, or schedule position. The upstream normalized Code Enforcement Officer record was incorrectly tagged as police; this validation corrects the candidate interpretation from the verbatim source line but does not overwrite the raw upstream record. The negative $0.91/hour documentary difference pertains only to these two specifically listed schedule values and must not be generalized to police and non-safety employees in Canastota. This is bounded local documentary evidence, not a final wage-gap estimate, and is not nationally representative. Substantive/manual validation of occupational and schedule-position comparability is required before analytic use beyond a qualified supporting example.

## Shreve, OH

Same-document hourly rates are validated, but duties, experience, hours, and other job requirements are not matched; use only as a bounded local documentary comparison.
