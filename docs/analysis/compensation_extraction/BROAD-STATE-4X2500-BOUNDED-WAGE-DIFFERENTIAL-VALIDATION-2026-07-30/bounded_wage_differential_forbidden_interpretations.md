# Forbidden interpretations

- Do not call any candidate a final wage-gap estimate.
- Do not treat these four municipalities as nationally representative or as population prevalence.
- Do not infer causality, policy effects, regression results, or treatment effects.
- Do not omit the rate type, period, compared occupations, or candidate-specific caveats.

- **Alburtis, PA:** Do not state that the Alburtis wage gap is 199.2%, that safety workers in Alburtis generally earned 199.2% more, that the comparison is representative, or that bargaining caused the difference. The evidence supports only a bounded, January-June 2018 documentary contrast between the named positions.
- **Cammack Village, AR:** Do not write 'Cammack Village's wage gap is 25%' or state that the patrolman actually earned 25% more. Do not infer a general safety premium, national prevalence, policy effect, representativeness, or causality from this maximum-schedule comparison.
- **Canastota, NY:** This validated candidate supports only a bounded local documentary statement about two listed hourly rates. It does not support a final wage-gap estimate, a citywide occupational average, a national or population-prevalence claim, a causal claim, or an assertion that the positions are equivalent.
- **Shreve, OH:** Do not write 'the wage gap is 37.5%' or infer a general safety premium, national prevalence, policy effect, or causal relationship.
