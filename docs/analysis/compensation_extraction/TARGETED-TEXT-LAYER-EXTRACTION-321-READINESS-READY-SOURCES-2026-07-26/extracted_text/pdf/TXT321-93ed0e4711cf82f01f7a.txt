RESOLUTION
WHEREAS, the current Collective Bargaining Agreement between the City of
Holland and the Holland Professional Firefighters Union, IAFF Local 759 will expire on
June 30, 2020; and,
WHEREAS, representatives of the IAFF and the City of Holland met in good faith
and negotiated a new Collective Bargaining Agreement; and,
.
WHEREAS, the new Collective Bargaining Agreement includes the following issues:
the addition of a subsection to the Use of Sick Leave procedure to allow for up to 24
hours of sick leave to care for a spouse or child (specifically following the birth of a child
and while covered under FMLA), adding Christmas Eve to the covered holidays,
modifying the Compensatory Time section, an increase to the full time shift firefighter’s
Food Allowance ($25/year), increased wages over the length of the Agreement (2.75%,
2.75%, 2.25%), increasing the Tuition Reimbursement benefit, changing the duration of
the contract from four (4) years to three (3) years, modifying the Bereavement Leave
section; and,
WHEREAS, the new Collective Bargaining Agreement between the Holland
Professional Firefighters Union, IAFF Local 759 and the City of Holland shall be
effective July 1, 2020 through June 30, 2023, and a copy of the CBA Settlement
Agreement is attached, which includes all changes to the current contract.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the new Collective Bargaining
Agreement between the City of Holland and the Professional Firefighters Union, IAFF
Local 759 is hereby adopted and the Mayor and Clerk are authorized to sign the new
Collective Bargaining Agreement on behalf of the City, subject to approval by the City
Attorney.

Resolution Declared Adopted.
Dated: June 3, 2020
Kathy Grimm, City Clerk
