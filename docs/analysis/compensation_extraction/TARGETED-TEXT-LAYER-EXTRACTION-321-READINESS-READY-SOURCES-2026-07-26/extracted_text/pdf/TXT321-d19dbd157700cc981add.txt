Memorandum of Understanding
Between The Board of Education of Mount Prospect School District 57
AND
The Mount Prospect Educational Association, IEA-NEA
Section 4-5 B
Duty-Free Lunch
This Memorandum of Understanding is entered between the Board of Education of Mount Prospect
School District (“Board”) and the Mount Prospect Educational Association, IEA-NEA (“Association”),
which are collectively referred to as “the parties.”
The parties’ current 2022-2027 collective bargaining agreement provides in Section 4-5B that, “All
Teachers are entitled to a duty-free lunch period as provided in the School Code. A duty-free lunch
of one hour at the elementary level and the length of one period at the middle level is provided to
teachers.”
The parties agree that beginning with the 2024-2025 school year, one period at the middle school
level shall be interpreted as 38 minutes except in the following situations:
1. Mondays in which the regular schedule allows for 35 minutes per lunch period;
2. During State and District-mandated testing; and, on days on which a special schedule is
necessary for operational or student learning purposes, lunch periods will be shortened only
after the following has occurred:
A. Staff Leadership Team (either building leadership team or team leaders) and
MPEA Leadership will be consulted to help develop the schedule or provide
input to a draft of a schedule.
B. For any schedule requiring a shortened lunch period, which will be defined as a
lunch period between 30-37 minutes, communication will be provided by
Administration to the Lincoln Staff as soon as possible, with 10 school days
prior notice when feasible.
“Although the above language is in acknowledgment of situations that may occur, Administration will
strive to avoid implementing schedules that include shortened lunch periods if it can be avoided and
still achieve the necessary programming for students. Accordingly, a “special schedule” shall be
permitted only in exceptional circumstances where operating under a 38-minute schedule or as
otherwise set forth herein is not reasonably feasible (e.g. an unforeseeable event such as a visit from a
dignitary or other unique opportunity).
Lunches will never be shortened below 30 minutes. Other than on Mondays, on days in which
lunches are shortened below 38 minutes, staff will be allowed to leave the building the
corresponding number of minutes before the typical end of the work day. For instance, if lunch is
30 minutes, staff can leave the building eight minutes before the typical staff end time.

Memorandum of Agreement
Between The Board of Education of Mount Prospect School District 57
AND
The Mount Prospect Educational Association, IEA-NEA
Section 5-2
Section 5-3
The parties’ current 2022-2027 collective bargaining agreement provides in Section 5-2 that,
“Insurance for non-renewal and retiring Teachers will cease on June 30th.” The parties to this
Memorandum of Agreement (“Agreement”) wish to modify that provision.
The parties agree that retirees will have an option of keeping their insurance through August 31 if
they choose. In this case, the final pay for retiring teachers will be on August 31 rather than June
30 as noted in Section 5-3.
This AGREEMENT supersedes any prior agreement(s) between the parties with respect to its subject
matter and constitutes a complete and exclusive statement of the terms of the agreement between
the parties with respect to its subject matter.
