On November 26, 1993, Milton-Freewater Education Association/OEA-OACE/NEA (Association) filed a petition under OAR
115-25-090(2) for certification as the exclusive representative
of the following bargaining unit:
All employees of the Milton-Freewater Unified
School District #7 who are required, as a
condition of employment, to possess an academic certificate, license, degree, or the
equivalent, issued by the State of Oregon, an
institution of higher education, or a professional society, excluding all supervisory,
confidential, and substitute employees.
The Association stated that the following school districts were merging, effective May 31, 1994:
Milton-Freewater Elementary School District 31
McLaughlin Union High School District 3
Ferndale School District 10
As its showing of interest, the Association submitted
excerpts from collective bargaining agreements entered into by
the Association's affiliates and each of the above districts. The
Association stated that there are 109 persons in the proposed
bargaining unit who are covered by those contracts. The Elections

Coordinator verified that the showing of interest submitted covered more than 50 percent of employees in the proposed unit.1
The Umatilla County Boundary Board approved the merger
(to be effective May 31, 1994) by resolution dated October 21,
1993. The petition was deemed timely.
The District filed objections to the proposed unit. The
parties subsequently agreed to the terms of the unit description
that we adopt in our Order.
order:

Based on the foregoing, the Board issues the following
ORDER

Milton-Freewater Education Association/OEA-OACE/NEA is
certified as the exclusive representative of the following bargaining unit of Milton-Freewater Unified School District #7:
All regular employees of the Milton-Freewater
Unified School District No. 7 who are
required, as a condition of employment, to
possess an academic certificate, license,
degree, or the equivalent, issued by the
State of Oregon, an institution of higher
education, or a professional society, excluding administrative, supervisory, confidential, substitute, and casual employees and
home visiting tutors.
