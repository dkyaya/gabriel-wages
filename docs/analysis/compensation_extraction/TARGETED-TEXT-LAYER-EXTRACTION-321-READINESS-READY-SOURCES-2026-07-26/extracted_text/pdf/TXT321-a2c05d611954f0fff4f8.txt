the Department is looking to hire part-time career staff to help provide staffing levels to ensure

increasingly harder to find across the country and this will help ensure they are able to continue

A motion was made by Daniel Ciciarelli to open the public hearing. The motion was seconded
Leesa Paul and was put to a roll call vote which resulted as follows: Hayley Downs: Yes, Leesa
Paul: Yes, Daniel Ciciarelli: Yes, David Carnie: Yes, Nicholas Paro: Yes.

Bill Files, Vice President of Teamsters Local 317, expressed opposition to the proposed change.
He informed the Board that they are opposed to the merger of the Parks Department being under
the supervision of the Highway Superintendent. He went on the explain that the Highway
Superintendent is in the supervisory collective bargaining agreement between the Town of Salina
and Teamsters Local 317 and that this proposal is a conflict under Taylor law and he encouraged
the Town Attorney to contact the Teamsters Local 317 legal counsel.
George Lowe, seasonal employee for the Town of Salina Parks department, praised the Parks
Department and Mark Newman, the Parks Maintenance Supervisor. He questioned the 1st, 2nd
and 4th Ward councilors as to whether, or not, they will be ready to deal with this change if the
Town Supervisor and 3rd Ward councilor are gone next year, pending election results. He also
asked the Board to explain why this change is being considered.
Supervisor Paro explained that the ultimate goal of this change is to transition the Parks
department into more of a recreation department. He affirmed him that the service at Salina
parks is not changing and employees will continue to do the same work that they currently do,
just under the supervision of the Highway Superintendent.
Councilor Ciciarelli reminded residents that they were welcome to attend budget meetings where
a lot of this was discussed. The Board agreed that this was the best path to take for the future of
the Town and hopes that the change will improve efficiencies and cut costs going forward. He
looks forward to expanding the recreation programs in the Town.
A motion was made by Supervisor Paro to close the public hearing. The motion was seconded
by Leesa Paul and was put to a roll call vote which resulted as follows: Hayley Downs: Yes,

The following resolution was moved, seconded and adopted:
WHEREAS, the Town of Salina Highway Superintendent (the “Highway
Superintendent”) has broad statutory powers to oversee a wide range of projects, including, the
employment of certain individuals to work on such projects, within the Town of Salina;
WHEREAS, pursuant to New York Town Law Section 32(1), the Highway
Superintendent “shall have such powers and shall perform such duties as are or hereafter may be
conferred or imposed upon him by law, and such further duties as the town board may determine
not inconsistent with law”;
WHEREAS, numerous New York State Comptroller Opinions, including, but not
limited to a 1982 Comptroller Opinion, have stated that the “the town board may, by resolution,
confer upon the [highway] superintendent the responsibility for the supervision of the department
of parks”;
WHEREAS, the Town Board believes that it is wise to confer upon the Highway
Superintendent the responsibility for the supervision of the department of parks for the following
reasons: (i) the parks department operates out of a portion of the highway facility, and, given the
park departments proximity to the highway facilities, and, the shared facilities, the highway
superintendent can better manage the day-to-day needs of the parks department, and (ii) giving

the Highway Superintendent supervisory responsibility over the parks department promotes
efficiencies within the Town of Salina by, among others, (a) increasing communications between
the departments, and (b) facilitating the sharing of resources amongst the departments.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT
RESOLVED, that the Highway Superintendent hereby is given the responsibility
for the supervision of the department of parks and the Town is hereby authorized to take such
further action as may be necessary to accomplish the foregoing, subject to final legal approval.

the CNYSPCA for additional Dog Control services for the remainder of 2024 in the amount of
$5,100.00 and 2025 in the amount of $24,640.00, pending legal review. The motion was
seconded by Leesa Paul and was put to a roll call vote which resulted as follows: Hayley Downs:
Yes, Leesa Paul: Yes, Daniel Ciciarelli: Yes, David Carnie: Yes, Nicholas Paro: Yes.
ADJOURNMENT
A motion was made by David Carnie and seconded by Leesa Paul to adjourn the meeting at
7:07p.m. All Voted in favor.
