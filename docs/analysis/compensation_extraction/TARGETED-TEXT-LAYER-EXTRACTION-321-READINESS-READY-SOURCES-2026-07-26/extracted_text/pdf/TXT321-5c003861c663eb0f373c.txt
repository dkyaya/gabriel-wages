SCHEDULE
Firefighters and Captains work under an FLSA Section 207(k) alternative work schedule that
24
is based on a 21 day work period with no Kelly Day in accordance with 29 CFR Sec. 553.230.
The work schedule consists of 24 hours on duty and 48 hours of duty. Firefighters are paid
for actual hours worked based on a 21 day work period. This regulation requires that
overtime compensation is due after 159 hours have been worked in a 21 day work period,
resulting in 9 hours of overtime every 21 days. Therefore, a work year consists of 2,764
regular hours plus 156 overtime hours, for a total work year of 2,920 hours. A 21 day work
period includes 3 payroll cycles, each with fluctuating regular and overtime hours; however,
the 3 payroll cycles yield the same number of regular hours and overtime hours.
