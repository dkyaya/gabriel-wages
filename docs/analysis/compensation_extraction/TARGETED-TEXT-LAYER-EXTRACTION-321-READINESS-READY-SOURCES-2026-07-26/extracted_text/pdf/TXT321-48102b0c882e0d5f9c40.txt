To see if the Town will vote to approve the Swampscott Retirement Board’s vote to increase the
cost of living base from $13,000 to $18,000 consistent with the provisions of Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 32,
Section 103, as amended by Chapter 188, Section 19, of the Acts of 2010, or take any action related thereto.
Sponsored by the Swampscott Retirement Board

ARTICLE 3.

Comment:
Retirees and survivors of the Swampscott Retirement System who are eligible to receive an annual
cost of living adjustment receive that adjustment based only on the first $13,000 of the retiree’s allowance and the cost of
living adjustment in any given year cannot exceed 3% by statute. Thus, in any given year the Swampscott Retirement
Board can only grant a $390 cost of living adjustment allowance. By increasing the base to $18,000, the Board has the
discretion to grant up to a 3% increase or an annual increase of $540. Accordingly, by voting to increase the base to
$18,000, each eligible retiree and survivor of the Swampscott Retirement System will receive at maximum an additional
$12.50 per month in their retirement allowance each year.
The Retirement Board’s actuary has calculated the change to the Retirement System’s Present Value of Future Benefits
(PVFB) to be approximately 2.3% of the existing liability, or $2 million. The PVFB is the cost of the system for current
employees, current retirees and beneficiaries if paid in a lump sum. However, similar to the effect of the change on the
retiree’s benefits, the cost of the benefit increase does not need to be paid all at once. Incorporating the additional cost
into the existing 15-year funding schedule, the Fiscal 2016 contribution would increase by about $200,000. Note that the
increase in the contribution amount is expected to increase by approximately 4.0% per year over that 15 year period.
Voted Article 3. That this Article is indefinitely postponed.

To see if the Town will vote to amend the Zoning By-Law, Section 4, by inserting the text
shown in bold and underlined which amendment will bring the Town into compliance with FEMA requirements, with the
existing text shown for informational purposes only
4.2.3.0. Location of District. The CFAOD is herein established as an overlay district. The CFAOD includes all special
flood hazard areas within the Town of Swampscott designated as AE, AO, or VE on the Essex County Flood Insurance
Rate Map (FIRM), issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) for the administration of the National
Flood Insurance Program (NFIP). The map panels of the Essex County FIRM that are wholly or partially within the Town
of Swampscott are panel number 25009C0531G, 25009C0532G, 25009C0533G and 25009C0534G dated July 16, 2014.
The exact boundaries of the CFAOD may be defined by the 100-year base flood elevations shown on the FIRM and
further defined by the Essex County Flood Insurance Study (FIS) report dated July 16, 2014. The FIRM and FIS report
are incorporated herein by reference and are on file with the Town Clerk, Planning Board, and Inspector of Buildings.
or take any action related thereto.

Sponsored by the Board of Selectmen and Planning Board
Comment:

FEMA has updated the flood zone maps for The Town of Swampscott. New or existing property
owners located in the previously defined or newly defined flood zones are typically required by
their lenders to obtain flood insurance through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP). In
order for the above mentioned property owners to obtain flood insurance through NFIP, the Town
must participate in the NFIP program. Adopting this zoning change will allow the Town to remain
compliant with the NFIP.
A non-related issue is property owners disputing the new maps. These requests can be sent to
the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) by the property owners privately hired
Professional Engineer or Land Surveyor by means of submitting an elevation certificate showing
that the structure’s sill elevation is above the base flood elevation.

Voted Article 18. That this Article is approved.
