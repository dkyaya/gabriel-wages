Resolution 2022-R-121
A resolution to approve a compensation increase for public safety employees
WHEREAS, the public safety employees of the City of Irondale provide vital services
for the citizens of the City of Irondale; and
WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Irondale desire to reward these employees
for their dedication and hard work through an increase to their compensation.
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of the City of Irondale that the
compensation of public safety municipal employees be increased in the amount of fourteen
percent (14%) beginning on October 5, 2022. A copy of this resolution shall be furnished by the
City Clerk to the Jefferson County Personnel Board.
ADOPTED & APPROVED: This 19th day of July, 2022.
