ORDINANCE NO. 2020 - 39
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING MISCELLANEOUS NUISANCES IN THE CITY OF
JASPER AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION OF SUCH REGULATIONS
(REPEALING ORDINANCE NO. 1997-14)
This ordinance made various changes to nuisance regulations and penalties for the City of Jasper,
repealing the previous ordinance. Members of the public, Alicen Ingle and Karla Neukem, spoke
about their experiences with the animal noises while residents of Jasper. City Attorney Renee
Kabrick explained some of the changes in greater detail. She also advised the ordinance will go
into effect April 1, 2021.
A motion was made by Council member John Schroeder and seconded by Council member Nancy
Eckerle to consider the ordinance for one reading only. Motion carried 7-0. Thereafter, a motion
was made by Council member Chad Lueken and seconded by Council member Paul Lorey to read
the ordinance by title only. Motion carried 7-0. The city attorney so read the ordinance.
Thereafter, a motion was made by Council member Kevin Manley and seconded by Council
member John Schroeder to pass and adopt Ordinance No. 2020-39. Motion carried 7-0 via roll call
vote.

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING ANIMALS IN THE CITY OF JASPER AND

(Amending Ordinance No. 2018-4)
This ordinance made various changes to the animal ordinance for the City of Jasper.

Eckerle to consider the ordinance for one reading only. Motion carried 7-0. Thereafter, a motion

RESOLUTION NO. 2020 - 16
RESOLUTION OF THE COMMON COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF JASPER, INDIANA
TO PROVIDE FOR REIMBURSEMENT OF PUBLIC HEALTH AND PUBLIC SAFETY
PAYROLL COSTS WITH CARES ACT FUNDING
This resolution is for the payment of the CARES Act funds for the public safety payroll. It
receipts the funds into the Jasper CARES Act fund and then transfers the funds to the general fund.
A motion was made by Council member Paul Lorey and seconded by Council member Chad
Lueken to consider the resolution for one reading only. Motion carried 7-0. Thereafter, a motion
was made by Council member Kevin Manley and seconded by Council member Nancy Eckerle to
read the resolution by title only. Motion carried 7-0. The city attorney so read the resolution.
Thereafter, a motion was made by Council member Kevin Manley and seconded by Council
member Nancy Eckerle to pass and adopt Resolution No. 2020-16. Motion carried 7-0 via roll call
vote.
RESOLUTION NO. 2020 - 15
A RESOLUTION OF THE COMMON COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF JASPER, INDIANA,
ADOPTING A CAPITAL ASSET POLICY FOR THE CIVIL CITY
This resolution is to increase the items for the capital asset policy from $500 to $1,500.
A motion was made by Council member Dave Hurst and seconded by Council member Paul Lorey
to consider the resolution for one reading only. Motion carried 7-0. Thereafter, a motion was
made by Council member Chad Lueken and seconded by Council member Paul Lorey to read the
resolution by title only. Motion carried 7-0. The city attorney so read the resolution. Thereafter, a
motion was made by Council member Chad Lueken and seconded by Council member John
Schroeder to pass and adopt Resolution No. 2020-15. Motion carried 7-0 via roll call vote.
TRANSFER OF PROPERTY
City Attorney Renee Kabrick advised the Council that there are parcels the City ofJasper and the
Greater Jasper School Corporation will need to swap in the area of the old 5th Street School. An
area is owned by the Park Commissioners. She asked the Council to authorize the Park Board to
swap the property.
A motion was made by Kevin Manley and seconded by Paul Lorey to authorize the Park Board to
take any action to swap the property at the old 5th Street School. Motion carried 7-0 via roll call
vote.
Adjournment. There being no further business to come before the board, a motion was made by
Council member Kevin Manley and seconded by Council member Dave Hurst to adjourn the meeting.
The motion carried 7-0 and the meeting adjourned at 6:48 p.m.
