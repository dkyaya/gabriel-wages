ORDER
The Kansas Public Employer-Employee Relations Board, has before it a Stipulated
Agreement signed by the Petitioner (AFSCME) and the Respondent (U.G.) to the abovecaptioned action.

Pursuant to the Stipulation Agreement, the Board makes the following

findings and conclusions:
1.

The Kansas Public Employer-Employee

Relations Board

issued a unit

determination on July 14, 1987, certifying that the American Federation of State, County,
Municipal Employees Union (AFSCME) Council 64, AFL-CIO, was designated as the exclusive
collective bargaining representative for three separate bargaining units of employees of the City
of Kansas City, Kansas.

Those bargaining units were designated as the Service and

Maintenance Unit, Clerical Unit, and Technical Unit.
2.

In the original unit determination and certification, certain classifications of

employees were listed as being employees to be represented by the exclusive collective

•

bargaining representative, AFSCME .

3.

•

The parties AFSCME and the Unified Government of Wyandotte County/Kansas

City, Kansas (U.G.), subsequent to the consolidation of the City of Kansas City, Kansas and
Wyandotte County, Kansas, have discussed the possible addition of certain employees who
were formally employed by Wyandotte County, Kansas to the referenced bargaining unit.
Those employees were employed as transportation workers for Wyandotte County, Kansas prior
to unification. They continue to be employed as transportation drivers of the Department of
Aging, but are now employed by the U.G ..
4.

AFSCME has acquired showing of interest cards from a great percentage of the

transportation drivers employed by the Department of Aging who are showing an interest in
becoming members of the AFSCME bargaining unit. Copies of these showing of interest cards
are attached hereto and marked as Exhibit A.
5.

The parties, after entering discussions concerning this matter, have agreed to

jointly stipulate and submit to PERB, for PERB's consideration, a request to amend the
certification of AFSCME to include these transportation drivers for the Department of Aging
within the AFSCME bargaining unit, specifically the Service and Maintenance Unit.
6.

AFSCME, upon the effective date of this Order, shall become the exclusive

collective bargaining representative of all transportation drivers for the Department of Aging
employed by the Unified Government. These employees, as of the effective date of this Order,
shall be covered by the Memorandum of Agreement between U.G. and AFSCME, and that the
only matter to be agreed upon between the parties is the appropriate wage rate for these new
members of the collective bargaining unit.
