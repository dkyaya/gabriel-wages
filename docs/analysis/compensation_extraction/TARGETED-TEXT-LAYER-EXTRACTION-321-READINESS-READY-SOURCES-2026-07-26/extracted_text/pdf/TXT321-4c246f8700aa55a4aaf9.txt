11. A MOTION to APPROVE the one-year contract extension for Building
Renovation, Restoration, and Reconstruction Services for use by all departments
with Abcore Restoration Company, Inc., at their quoted and adjusted bid prices.

The Town utilizes two separate contracts for general construction work. The first contract,
General Construction Services is for typical routine and emergency building trades work at all
Town buildings. This bid for “Building Renovation, Restoration, and Reconstruction Services” is
for specialty projects and buildings of historical or other noteworthy significance (like the
Towers). The bid encompasses carpentry, painting, flooring, and roofing trades at fixed hourly
labor rates (State prevailing wage minimums) on a regular and emergency response basis.
Please note that State law requires the payment of prevailing wages for all public contracts in
excess of $1,000.00. Response times, equipment mark-ups, and material mark-ups are also
included in the bid. The contract may be used as needed by all Town departments. Town
Council awarded the original bid on March 7, 2016. Abcore has agreed to extend the
contract for one year at the adjusted hourly rates to meet the State of Rhode Island prevailing
wages requirements as follows: Carpentry: $62.43; Painting: $53.17; Flooring: $62.43; and
Roofing: $58.37. The one-year contract extension is March 7, 2017 – March 6, 2018 with all of
the same terms and conditions as the original bid specifications. Any project initiated under this
contract with a value exceeding $4,000.00 requires prior Town Council authorization. In
addition, work under this contract will be limited to a value of less than $50,000.00 unless further
Town Council approval has been granted. Funding is available in the respective

14. A MOTION to APPROVE the addition of a new Beach Security Officer position
for the Town beach for the 2018 season.

The Parks and Recreation Department is requesting the addition of a new seasonal position for
the beach as a “Beach Security Officer” to replace the Community Service Officers presently
performing security responsibilities during the day shift at the beach. The Beach Security Officer
Position proposed wage range is $12.00 to $15.00 and will be hired to work days 11:00 am to
7:00 pm. from the third Saturday in June through Labor Day reporting directly to Beach
Management. Three Beach Security Officers will be hired, one for weekdays and two for

the applicant’s background and ability. The Community Service Officers would continue to work
the night shift from Memorial Day through Labor Day to provide security responsibilities at the
beach to check buildings and close the parking lot gates while reporting directly to the Police
Department. Beach Police Protection Account # 0034-50216 is presently $42,000.00. The
Department will utilize up to $15,000.00 to cover costs related to daytime beach security officer
shifts at the beach.

16. A MOTION to ADOPT a RESOLUTION authorizing the Town Manager to
submit a request for a 2017 Community Development Block Grant for $75,000
from the State set-aside for local housing rehabilitation and RECEIVE and
PLACE on file the recommendation from the Planning Board.
Due to constraints on funding availability, at this time, the RI Office of Housing and Community
Development is soliciting local CDBG submittals limited to housing rehabilitation grants only, to
be funded from the State “set-aside”. Unique to this request for “set-aside” funds is that there
are no specific applicants for the funds. If awarded, the State will make available these funds on
a case-by-case basis to assist income-qualified homeowners, dependent upon local criteria.
After conferring with the Town’s CDBG consultant, Geoff Marchant of the East Greenwich
Housing Consortium, the Town staff suggests a request of $75,000. If received this grant will
not detract from the Town’s allowance to request up to $150,000 when the general grant
application period opens.
Under State law a public hearing is required before a Town may apply for a CDBG housing
rehab grant. A hearing for all eight communities represented by the East Greenwich Housing
consortium was coordinated and conducted by Mr. Marchant on November 1, 2017 at the
Richmond Town Hall. On December 12, 2017, the Planning Board considered the application
and recommended its approval. Town Council has final authority to consider if this program is
worthy of support. Your final decision must be made via resolution in order that the staff may
promptly assemble and submit the Town’s request to the State Office of Housing and
Community Development.
