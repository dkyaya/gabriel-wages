No Parking Zone – Virginia Drive
This office has received a request to investigate placing a no parking zone on Virginia Drive
from Union Road to the terminus in an effort to prohibit drop-off of students and the subsequent
unsafe vehicular movements on this short, dead end street with no cul-de-sac. The Police
Department and this office investigated this request and it was determined that parking should be
prohibited on both sides of the street from Union Road to the terminus from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00
p.m., Monday through Friday from September through June. (An ordinance must be adopted
by the Township Council).

A. RESOLUTION
1. R2021-165 Approving Settlement in All Carifi Matters

WHEREAS, the Township of Parsippany-Troy Hills (the “Township”) is a party to
litigation bearing docket number James Carifi v. Township of Parsippany-Troy Hills, et. al.,
Docket No. MRS-L-2938-11 (the “Carifi I”); and
WHEREAS, PBA Local 131A, Superior Officers’ Association (“SOA”) filed an
arbitration alleging violations of the collective bargaining agreement as it related to the
provisions governing payment of unused leave balances to Mr. Carifi, which is captioned before
the Public Employment Relations Commission as Township of Parsippany-Troy Hills and PBA
Local 131A SOA, PERC Docket No. AR-2019-325 (the “Leave Balance Arbitration”); and
WHEREAS, the SOA filed a grievance against the Township, PERC Docket No. AR2021-151 (“Letter of Good Standing Arbitration”) alleging that it breached the collective
bargaining agreement, and well-established past practice, by refusing to provide Police Captain
Carifi with a letter confirming he retired in good standing; and
WHEREAS, the SOA filed a Step 2 grievance (“Legal Fee Indemnification
Arbitration”) against the Township alleging that it has refused Carifi’s request for
reimbursement of his legal defense costs arising from work-related administrative and criminal
charges that have been filed; and
WHEREAS, the Leave Balance Arbitration, Letter of Good Standing Arbitration and
Legal Fee Indemnification Arbitration shall collectively be referred to herein as the PERC
Claims; and
WHEREAS, at the direction of the Mayor and Township Council, Special Litigation
Counsel for the Township and the Township Attorney have engaged in settlement negotiations
with the attorney for Mr. Carifi; and
WHEREAS, said negotiations have resulted in a comprehensive settlement of all
matters, including Carifi I and the PERC Claims, in exchange for a settlement amount not to
exceed One Million Dollars ($1,000,000), as allocated between the Township and the Morris
County Municipal Joint Insurance Fund and Garden State Municipal Joint Insurance Fund, and
their respective excess insurance carriers, and as will be more particularly set forth in a form of
Release and Settlement Agreement that is substantially similar to the one attached here and will
be between Mr. Carifi, the Township and the SOA, in its role as grievant in the PERC Claims;
and

Page 11

Agenda Meeting 11/9/21
WHEREAS, the aforementioned settlement is contingent upon the SOA’s dismissal of
the PERC Claims, with prejudice; and
WHEREAS, the Mayor and Township Council have been briefed on the status of all
Carifi-related matters involving litigation, insurance coverage and the like; and
WHEREAS, the Morris County Municipal Joint Insurance Fund and Garden State
Municipal Joint Insurance Fund have reviewed and approved the settlement reflected in the
proposed form of Release and Settlement Agreement; and
WHEREAS, the Mayor and Township Council have reviewed the settlement reflected in
the proposed form of Release and Settlement Agreement and wish to approve same; and
WHEREAS, the Mayor and Township Council have determined that said settlement is in
the best interest of the Township and avoids the burden, expense, delay and uncertainties of
further litigation and/or the continuance of the PERC Claims.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Township Council of the Township
of Parsippany-Troy Hills, in the County of Morris, and State of New Jersey, that the above
recitals are hereby incorporated by reference and the settlement is hereby approved in
accordance with the terms set forth in the attached proposed form Release and Settlement
Agreement. The Mayor and Township Clerk are hereby authorized to execute said Release and
Settlement Agreement.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the Township Council hereby authorizes and approves
any modifications to legal language of the proposed form of Release and Settlement Agreement
as may be recommended and approved by Special Litigation Counsel for the Township and the
Township Attorney prior to execution.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Special Litigation Counsel for the Township is
hereby authorized and directed to effect said settlement.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that all Township officials and employees are hereby
authorized and directed to take all action necessary and appropriate to effectuate the terms of this
Resolution.
Motion to approve the Resolution above by Council member Gragnani, seconded
by Council member Peterson.
