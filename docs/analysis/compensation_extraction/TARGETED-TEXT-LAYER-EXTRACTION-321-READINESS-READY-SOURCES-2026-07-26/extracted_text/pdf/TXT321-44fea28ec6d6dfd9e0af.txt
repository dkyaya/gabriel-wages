On October 27, 2023, the City of Bellaire engaged Paypoint HR, LLC ("Paypoint") to conduct a
classification study and total compensation study for the City. Paypoint completed the studies and
will be presenting its findings to City Council for consideration and discussion.

Engaged employees and managers with job analysis questionnaires to better understand
(beyond what is contained in job descriptions) the knowledge, skills, and abilities required
for each position. Manager reviews served as a checks and balance for the process.
The 9 compensable factors from the questionnaires (education, impact, supervision, etc.)
were used to create the first yardstick.

External Competitiveness Yardstick (Compensation)
•

•
•

Comparator organizations were selected that are socio-economically similar to Bellaire. [As
percentiles are used rather than average, single responses do not falsely skew results.]
Surveys distributed to comparators that included a brief summary of the requirements of the
position at Bellaire to ensure an apples-to-apples comparison.
Results of the market survey were used to develop the second yardstick.

Org Structure Yardstick
•

In consultation with the project team, a third yardstick was developed that considered the
organizational structure of Bellaire (directors, managers, supervisors, leads, etc.).

Final step was to statistically combine the three independent
measures/yardsticks to develop a comprehensive
understanding of what fair pay looks like for Bellaire.

Employee Focus Groups
Key Takeaways from Employee Concerns and Suggestions
Page 20 of 30

1. Job Descriptions and Position Review:
• Updates needed for clarity and to better set expectations.
• Select positions require review for possible reclassification / reassignment.
2. Establishment of Citywide Goals and Values:
• Input from staff required for setting citywide goals, mission, vision, and core values.
• Current service level expectations are exceptionally high compared to peers.
• Consistent communication of goals citywide suggested.
• Goals should be tied to performance metrics for meaningful impact.
3. Economic and Financial Considerations:
• Local economy thriving with high home values.
• Limited zoned areas for business.
• Need for commercial development to boost revenue.
• Non-profit tax-exempt entities owning large land parcels.
• Rising living costs affecting staff residency & commutes.
• Increase fees and taxes for infrastructure sustainability.
4. Regulatory Compliance and Enforcement:
• Adherence to and enforcement of regulations critical.
• Impact of state and federal regulations on city resources highlighted.
• City ordinances require careful planning and drafting for enforceability

Employee Focus Groups page 2

4. Culture and Support:
• Positive internal culture; described as improving with a "family" feel.
• Strong public support with substantial citizen contributions.
• Value seen in tools, technology, and facility improvements. Need for improved
facilities in Public Works, Library, and Parks. Fleet improvements suggested. More
timely availability of common tools & equipment.
• Address staffing levels, SOPs, and training needs.
• Allow staff to expand public education of services.
5. Career Development & Compensation:
• Currently limited career growth due to organizational structure.
• Suggest additional job levels with desire for recognition of certifications and
responsibilities.
• Need for departmental administrative support.
• Issues with compensation step plan adherence, pay compression, and COLA
increases.
o Lack of step increases impacting morale and turnover.
o Pay compression affecting tenured staff.
o Desire for recognition based on performance; sporadic COLA increases not
keeping up with inflation.

STAFF RECOMMENDATIONS
 Adjust pay to ensure employee compensation is benchmarked to
Page 30 of 30

50% of market ($333,969 budget impact). Does not include city
benefit and retirement contributions.

 Adopt a range pay plan to reduce compression, retain great

employees, and improve morale.

 Annually budget a merit pool based on city finances, with a

separate COLA based on established formula.

 Create job families for employee development, retention, and

compensation transparency.

 Issue an RFP for medical benefits and evaluate premiums and

copays to ensure they stay competitive.

 Offer benefits that improve morale with negligible/no impact on

customer service and budget (examples: floating holidays,
scheduled closures, flexible work schedules, remote work).

 Focus on organizational health and workplace culture.

 Establish purpose statement and core values.
 Hire for and award merit pay based on demonstrated
commitment to core values.
