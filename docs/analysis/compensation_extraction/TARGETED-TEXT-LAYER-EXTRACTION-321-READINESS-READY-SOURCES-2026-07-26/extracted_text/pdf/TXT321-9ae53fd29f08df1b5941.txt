THE PROJECT
The Town of Lewisville is seeking proposals from qualified firms to provide classification and
compensation study services to the Town. We anticipate having a Classification &
Compensation system that is logical, understandable and equitable.
We are interested in a process that includes employee engagement in an evaluation of our
system. The Town’s objectives are to:

Study and evaluate all positions within the Town for purposes of determining proper
classification and salary for each employee;
Standardize and modernize job titles and job descriptions;
Identify those classes of positions that are exempt and non-exempt in compliance with
the Fair Labor Standards Act;
Conduct employee information gathering sessions;
Develop and present final recommendations and implementation plan including impact
of implementing recommended adjustments to current salaries both immediately and in
the future; and
Make recommendations to the Town Manager about any changes to compensation
system.

INTRODUCTION
The Town of Lewisville is a small town nestled in Western Forsyth County with a population of
approximately 13,500 residents. Lewisville prides itself on small town charm and community.
The Town operates under a Manager/Council form of government with only 13 full-time
employees to deliver quality services to the community.

SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Town of Lewisville invites all qualified personnel management consultants to submit
proposals to:
1. Perform a review of the existing classification plan,
2. Provide a comprehensive evaluation of every job within the Town to determine relative
worth within the organization for the establishment of pay ranges and step progressions
within the ranges,
3. Simplify or create job titles and job descriptions and align all jobs into job families with
similar roles and functions; and that may reflect a similar level of education and skills
and/or tasks,
4. Analyze and recommend changes to the present compensation structure to meet
market analysis. This may include recommendations for individual positions as well.
5. Review job descriptions and place them in the compensation structure at the
appropriate level,
6. Identify potential pay compression issues and provide potential solutions, and
7. Review current staffing patterns to ensure that the organization is properly staffed for
effectiveness and efficiency.
TASK 1: Information Meetings
1. Consultant to schedule an initial meeting with the Town Manager to discuss the process
and tasks to be performed in the study to include reasonable dedication of key
personnel.
2. Consultant to meet with Town Manager and department heads to explain study and
process to be used.
3. Consultant to provide at lease bi-weekly updates to the Town Manager.
TASK 2: Classification Study
1. Consultant to review current classification grade methodology and propose
recommended strategies for the town.
2. Consultant to conduct interviews and/or job audits with each employee.
3. Consultant to update job descriptions to uniformly reflect the distinguishing
characteristics, essential job functions, minimum qualifications (education/experience
and knowledge/skills/abilities), working conditions (physical demands, work
environment, and travel requirements), and certification/licenses/registration

TASK 3: Compensation Study
1. Consultant to review current compensation plan (salary grade levels) and understand
current challenges in recruiting and retaining employees.
2. Consultant to recommend and identify a consistent and competitive market position
that the Town can strive to maintain.
3. Consultant to recommend comparable labor markets, including both private and public
sector employees for compensation study.
4. Consultant to recommend appropriate salary range for each position based on the
classification plan, internal relationships and equality. Prepare a new salary structure
based on best practices.
5. Consultant to identify any extreme current individual or group compensation inequities
and to provide a recommended corrective action plan and process to remedy these
situations.
6. Consultant to conduct a compression analysis to include any recommendations for
implementation.
Town Resources
The Town will provide access to staff, copies of all pay ranges, job classifications, and any other
available in-house information requested by the selected consultant that may be required to
complete the study.

Final selection and contracting will be subject to approval by the Town of Lewisville. All firms

costs related to this presentation. The Selection Committee will make a recommendation to
the Town Council who may then award the contract to the responsible firm with the most
advantageous proposal, taking into consideration price, cost effectiveness, comprehensiveness,
and project approach.
