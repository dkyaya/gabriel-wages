Swearing In Ceremony for Police Officers
Chief Hughes came forward to swear in the newest members of the Hamilton Township Police
Department. Mr. Connor Grisso came forward first. He was a graduate of Lakota West High
school and then enlisted into the United States Army where he served four years and was
honorably discharged as an E5 Sargent. Earlier this year he decided he wanted to be a Police
Officer and enrolled in the Butler Tech Peace Officer Academy. On day 3 we had our first
interaction together, he had great promise from the moment we met him. His girlfriend Erica did
the honors of pinning on his badge. Mr. Sousa came forward to give the Oath to Connor.
Chief Hughes then swore in Mr. Ben Rossenberger. He spent five years with the Ohio State
Highway Patrol out of post 83 in Lebanon. He attended Wright State University where he
worked as a junior high basketball coach at Centerville Schools. During his time as a State
Trooper he received several awards including the Criminal Patrol Award, Fitness Award and the
Safe Driving Award. His wife Casey did the honors of pinning on his badge. Mr. Sousa came
forward to give the Oath to Ben.

Resolution 23-0607B: Increase in appropriation in the Fire and PMVL Funds

Mr. Pegram explained that the resolution and two motions to follow are all related. The
resolution is to increase appropriations in the Fire & Ambulance budget as well as the PMVL
budget. These are for an ambulance and salt truck we plan on purchasing next year. Due to the
shortage of vehicles and the long time it is taking to receive the vehicles if we order them in June
of this year we should be able to receive them by 2024. If we wait till 2024 it may not be
received until 2025 or 2026.

- Motion – Motion to approve 2024 – 2026 Collective Bargaining Agreement with IAFF Local
4055
Mr. Pegram stated we reached an agreement with the IAFF Local 4055 which represents our Full
Time Firefighters. They have already voted and approved. It’s a 3 year contract. We had a great
negotiation. Currently our full time EMT’s pay rate is very close to the Paramedic pay rate.
Typically there is a larger gap to incentivize people to go to Paramedic School. What we did with
contract is lower the EMT pay to give us that incentive. It is still a competitive rate. The first
item is the actual agreement. The second is entering into an MOU with the Union that allows us
to higher new employees at this rate in 2023 because the contact doesn’t go into effect until
2024.
Mr. Sousa made a motion with a second from Mr. Rozzi to approve 2024 – 2026 Collective
Bargaining Agreement with IAFF Local 4055.
