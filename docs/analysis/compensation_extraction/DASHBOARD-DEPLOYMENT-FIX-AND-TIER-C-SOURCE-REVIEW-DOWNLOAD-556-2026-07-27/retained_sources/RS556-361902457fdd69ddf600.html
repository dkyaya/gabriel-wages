Union Contracts

Collective Bargaining Agreements

The Town of Dracut maintains several collective bargaining agreements (CBAs) with municipal labor groups. These contracts establish the terms and conditions of employment for unionized staff and serve as the foundation for collaborative labor–management relationships.

The agreements outline essential information such as:

  • Wages and step schedules
  • Working hours, overtime provisions, and shift differentials
  • Leave entitlements and benefits
  • Grievance and arbitration procedures
  • Health, safety, and workplace conditions
  • Department-specific operational provisions

These contracts are periodically renegotiated to ensure they remain current, equitable, and aligned with municipal needs.
