# Ordinance, budget, and pay-plan formalization

**Internal finding.** Council action, ordinances, budgets, pay plans, and classification systems translate compensation proposals into funded policy, while also imposing fiscal limits.

**Fit with the safety-wage-growth assertion:** `partially_supports`.

**Strength:** `moderate`.

**Examples:** WCCPEX-84d60d648789abeaa588e12c, WCCPEX-691b60a557d81d806bb055be, WCCPEX-6e0a5f498b3ab5fea3275c6c, WCCPEX-9484c5ee48320bc2c57fca84, WCCPEX-c24569c6bd53381699bae3b1, WCCPEX-caedb9df9638617877d52c61

**Limits.** Non-safety mechanisms are real; local matched comparisons remain partial; mechanism coding is not a frequency or effect-size estimate.

**Good-as-gold evidence.** Repeated same-city, same-period, same-cycle safety/non-safety contracts with harmonized pay basis, comparable roles, total-compensation components, and a credible panel or shock-based identification design.

**Boundary.** Internal bounded mechanism claim; no national prevalence, wage-gap estimate, or causal-effect estimate.
