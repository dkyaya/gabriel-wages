# Non-safety mechanisms as counterweights

**Internal finding.** Non-safety units also receive bargaining, steps, COLAs, and pay-plan mechanisms; the defensible distinction is a reinforcing safety pressure bundle, not a claim that non-safety compensation lacks institutional growth channels.

**Fit with the safety-wage-growth assertion:** `complicates`.

**Strength:** `moderate`.

**Examples:** WCCPEX-fa637add09963a080b3734e2, WCCPEX-c85689d111d6649da0624c40, WCCPEX-f2fd6f6f10b7f68fc611502d, WCCPEX-04b78c315901ed753adfaafc, WCCPEX-b7717536d2561f66db0ced52, WCCPEX-620023a75d044a4d722b9ff0, WCCPEX-80dbbcb3f4d246dc7abfaaef, WCCPEX-50ce521ead120927aeb6428f

**Limits.** Non-safety mechanisms are real; local matched comparisons remain partial; mechanism coding is not a frequency or effect-size estimate.

**Good-as-gold evidence.** Repeated same-city, same-period, same-cycle safety/non-safety contracts with harmonized pay basis, comparable roles, total-compensation components, and a credible panel or shock-based identification design.

**Boundary.** Internal bounded mechanism claim; no national prevalence, wage-gap estimate, or causal-effect estimate.
