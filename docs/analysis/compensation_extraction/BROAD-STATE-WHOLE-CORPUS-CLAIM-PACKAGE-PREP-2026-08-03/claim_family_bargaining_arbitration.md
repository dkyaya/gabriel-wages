# Formal bargaining, arbitration, and factfinding

**Internal finding.** Safety compensation is exposed to recurring formal negotiation and impasse-resolution institutions that can lift wage floors, preserve leverage, and lock increases into agreements or awards.

**Fit with the safety-wage-growth assertion:** `supports`.

**Strength:** `moderate`.

**Examples:** WCCPEX-b7717536d2561f66db0ced52, WCCPEX-620023a75d044a4d722b9ff0, WCCPEX-c50f28536893cd93aa5a787c, WCCPEX-9002228502ce09035b47f850, WCCPEX-f9fc40ff1c1507df5ef0c2fc, WCCPEX-4c27a8c5b48512597d9835de

**Limits.** Non-safety mechanisms are real; local matched comparisons remain partial; mechanism coding is not a frequency or effect-size estimate.

**Good-as-gold evidence.** Repeated same-city, same-period, same-cycle safety/non-safety contracts with harmonized pay basis, comparable roles, total-compensation components, and a credible panel or shock-based identification design.

**Boundary.** Internal bounded mechanism claim; no national prevalence, wage-gap estimate, or causal-effect estimate.
