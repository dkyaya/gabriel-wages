# Quantitative claim boundaries

A claim-ready row may support only a statement that its preserved text directly reports the recorded wage, rate, raise, premium, schedule, step, grade, or effective-date value. Prior normalized helper fields are retained as lineage but are not generated, modified, or promoted here. This task performs no imputation, destructive normalization, annualization, comparison, wage-gap calculation, regression, treatment-effect estimate, population-prevalence inference, national generalization, or causal analysis.
