Signed and Sealed | 2025

Posted by TPOAM on January 8, 2025 under Signed & Sealed

SIGNED AND SEALED 2025 | #6

Local Association Name: Milan Supervisors Association
Duration: 3 years; July 2025 – June 2028

WAGE INCREASES

  • July 2026: +3%
  • July 2027: +3%
  • July 2028: +3%

Other Important Issues

  • Employer pays 100% of retirement (single family) health care for the employee only.
  • Employer pays for 8-week maternity leave.
  • Employer increases boot allowance to $300.
  • All holidays will be paid holiday overtime if called in on a holiday.
  • Employees will be issued winter work gear.
  • Add certifications for which the employer will pay 1% increase or a stipend. Employer will pay for 1 re-test if the employee fails.
  • City will pay $3,500 for an insurance waiver.

The bargaining team included Jon Fitch and Karis Dye, whom Business Agent Harry Valentine assisted.

SIGNED AND SEALED 2025 | #5

Local Association Name: Imlay City Department of Public Works
Duration: 4 years; July 2025 – June 2029
Mediator’s Name: Richard Ziegler

WAGE INCREASES

  • July 2025: +3%
  • July 2026: +3.5%
  • July 2027: +3.5%
  • July 2028: +3.5%

Other Important Issues

  • $500 signing bonus.
  • Condensed wage scale from 25-year top pay to 20-year top pay.
  • Increase optical benefit.
  • 2% employer contribution to 457 plan.

The bargaining team included Casey Linck, who Business Agent Michael Gerald assisted.

SIGNED AND SEALED 2025 | #3

Local Association Name: Milford Department of Public Works (DPW)
Duration: 3 years; 2024 – 2026
Mediator’s Name: Mikie Czerniak

WAGE INCREASES

  • 2024: +3%
  • 2025: +3%
  • 2026: +3%

Other Important Issues

  • Retained longevity for Tier 1 employees.
  • Boot allowance changed to every 12 months.
  • Updated language for tuition reimbursement and training.
  • Changed wages for promotions to include language for at 5% increase from the current step.
  • Increased certification pay by $100 and another $100 in 2026.

The bargaining team consisted of Steven Degnan and Josiah Vincent, who were assisted by Business Agent Eric Ronewicz.

SIGNED AND SEALED 2025 | #2

Local Association Name: 38th Circuit Court Family Division Juvenile Youth Home Supervisors Association
Duration: 2 years; January 2025 – December 2026

WAGE INCREASES

  • 2025: +3%
  • 2026: +3%
  • Compressed wage steps
  • Signing Bonus: Retention bonus each year of the contract.
    • $1,000 to $2,000 each year, depending on employees years of service.

Other Important Issues

  • Add Juneteenth as a paid holiday.
  • Modify health care language, keeping coverage the same.
  • Employer increases pension contribution each year from 7% to 9%.
  • Funeral leave must be taken two (2) from the date of death.
  • Employer increases payment into health savings account to 8%.

The bargaining team consisted of Austin Wickenhiser who was assisted by Business Agent Harry Valentine.

SIGNED AND SEALED 2025 | #1

Local Association Name: Harper Woods Department of Public Safety (TPOAM)
Duration: 3 years; January 2025 – December 2027

WAGE INCREASES

  • 2025: +4%
  • 2026: +4%
  • 2027: +4%
  • Signing Bonus: $1,000 per employee

Other Important Issues

  • $3 per hour, base wage increase for mechanics.
  • Three (3) additional funeral leave days.

The bargaining team consisted of President David Ciechanowski and Vice President Willie Anderson, who were assisted by Business Agent Michael Gerald.
