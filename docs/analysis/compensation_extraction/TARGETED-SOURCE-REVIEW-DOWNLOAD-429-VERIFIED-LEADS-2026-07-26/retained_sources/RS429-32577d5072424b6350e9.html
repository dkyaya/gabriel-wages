Fiscal and Governance Transparency

Overview

The Town of Bedford strives for transparency in our fiscal management and government process. The below items are considered by many to be key elements in providing "sunshine" on the inner workings of local government. Other records of the Town may be requested following the FOIL request process. 

All public boards and commissions are subject to the Open Meetings Law, publicly noticed, and the public is welcome to attend.
