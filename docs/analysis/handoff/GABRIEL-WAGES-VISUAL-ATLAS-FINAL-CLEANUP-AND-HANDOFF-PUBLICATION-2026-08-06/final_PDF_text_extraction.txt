How to read a hex map
A hex map groups nearby municipalities into equal-sized areas so regional
patterns are easier to see. Darker areas contain more distinct documented
compensation events. The shading does not represent more employees, a larger
wage effect, or how common the mechanism is nationwide. Alaska uses a
separate inset scale.
