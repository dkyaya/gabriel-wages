## What the evidence supports

The reviewed records show several concrete ways municipal compensation can change: bargaining and arbitration, scheduled increases, non-base pay, staffing and market pressure, retroactive implementation, budgets, pay plans, and council action. The strongest conclusion is a bounded account of these processes, not a national estimate of how much faster safety wages grow.

The final claim set contains one supported claim, one conditionally supported claim, five claims supported only as compensation mechanisms, one mixed finding, and six unsupported national, regression, or causal claims. Safety-side events appear more often in the retained and successfully classified evidence, but the corpus also contains more readily identifiable safety documentation. That imbalance is relevant to interpretation; it does not prove that the same imbalance exists nationally or that it caused a wage difference.
