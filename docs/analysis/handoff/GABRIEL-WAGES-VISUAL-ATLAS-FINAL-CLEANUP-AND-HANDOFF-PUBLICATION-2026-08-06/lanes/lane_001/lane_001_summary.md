## Key editorial decisions

1. **Public title:** *Why Public-Safety Wages May Grow Differently: A Visual Atlas of Municipal Compensation Evidence.* The title poses the research question and does not imply that a national wage gap was estimated.
2. **Opening result:** Lead with the bounded mechanism account. State the final distribution of one supported claim, one conditional claim, five mechanism-only claims, one mixed finding, and six unsupported claims.
3. **Reader-guide limit:** Use exactly three guide pages. The glossary follows separately and precedes the first analytical visual.
4. **Plain analytical language:** Use “distinct implementation events,” “retained evidence,” and “unsearched targets.” Remove public references to editing passes, handoff status, workers, queues, tasks, or repairs.
5. **Side imbalance:** Describe the higher frequency of safety-side evidence as a pattern in retained and successfully classified records. Do not present it as national prevalence or causal evidence.
6. **Map boundary:** Define hex shading once as documented implementation-event concentration, not workers, effect size, or prevalence.
7. **Implementation boundary:** Preserve the distinction among proposal, adoption, payroll-effective status, and observed payment. “No paid stage observed” never becomes “never paid.”
8. **Two-corpus rule:** Keep governing documentary evidence separate from explanatory discourse evidence in both terminology and method descriptions.
