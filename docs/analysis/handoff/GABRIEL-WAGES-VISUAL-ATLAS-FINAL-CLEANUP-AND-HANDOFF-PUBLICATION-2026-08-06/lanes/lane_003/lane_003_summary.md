## Key editorial decisions

1. **Corpus units stay separate.** Files, pages, documents, tables, rows, payloads, events, mechanism links, municipalities, claims, and conflicts cannot share one quantitative scale or be added into a grand total.

2. **Side visibility uses within-layer denominators.** The final data preserve safety, non-safety, mixed, side-independent, and unresolved categories. A classified-side inset may use `safety + non-safety` as its denominator, but it must also show the unresolved share in the full-layer panel.

3. **Local comparisons are two-sided.** The inherited `mixed` category for all ten comparison records is retained for lineage, but the reader-facing visual should call them ten two-sided local comparisons.

4. **Claims need two pages.** CLAIM-A through CLAIM-H occupy the first matrix page; the six unsupported propositions occupy the second. Full wording, linked-record counts, class, evidence tiers, and boundaries remain readable.

5. **Evidence counts are not weights.** Exact links, tier counts, counterexamples, and conflicts describe different units. They cannot be summed or interpreted as comparable evidence strength. The 201 conflicts are a global unlinked hold, not 201 conflicts per claim.

6. **Counterexamples remain seven.** The canonical link table has 14 rows because the same seven records link to CLAIM-G and CLAIM-H. The atlas total remains seven unique counterexamples.

7. **Cross-mechanism material is relocated, not dropped.** The divider and five redundant standalone pages are removed from the prior plan. Growth, staffing, implementation, local comparisons, and counterexamples move beside the mechanisms or claims they interpret. Zero analytical visuals are discarded.

## Canonical preservation

All 14 classes remain unchanged: one supported, one conditionally supported, five mechanism-supported only, one mixed or countervailing, and six unsupported. All 14 final claim texts match the adjudicated table exactly. Seven unique counterexamples and the 201-record global conflict hold remain explicit.

The only remaining work is coordinator rendering and page-level visual QA.
