# Original map-layout diagnosis

The first atlas had a substantive rendering failure. Its y-axis stopped at +1.7 million projected meters while the lower-48 basemap extends beyond +3.17 million. Across category views, 5,179 of 6,338 lower-48 hex rows and 11,840 of 13,342 represented event counts fell outside the frame; 49 Alaska rows were also omitted. The canonical records were present. The revised maps derive one fixed extent from the full basemap and add a labeled Alaska inset.
